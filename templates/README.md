# Story templates

Templates control **structure and pacing**, never facts. The `template` field in `episode.json` must be one of the three ids below. Each template lists the beats a scene plan should hit; the research record decides what is true.

## `hidden-mechanism` — THE HIDDEN MECHANISM
An everyday system has a surprising underlying mechanism.
1. **Hook (0–3s):** the ordinary thing, framed as a question. *"Why does X happen every single day?"*
2. **The visible layer (3–12s):** what everyone sees.
3. **Descent (12–30s):** the explorer goes inside/under/behind; the mechanism appears piece by piece.
4. **The mechanism (30–48s):** animated diagram; one clear causal chain.
5. **Consequence (48–58s):** what breaks when it fails, or what it quietly makes possible.
6. **Takeaway (58–65s):** one line you'd repeat to a friend.

## `cascade` — THE CASCADE
A small disruption spreads through a larger system.
1. **Hook:** the tiny trigger next to the enormous outcome.
2. **Normal day:** the system working, briefly.
3. **First domino:** the small failure, exactly as documented.
4. **Propagation:** each hop shown as a link in a network; pace quickens.
5. **The pause:** the moment it could have stopped, and why it didn't.
6. **Aftermath + lesson:** what changed afterwards (from the investigation report).
Respect for victims: no jokes at the point of harm; humour lives in the setup, not the loss.

## `counterintuitive-truth` — THE COUNTERINTUITIVE TRUTH
Something appears impossible or contradictory until the underlying principle is revealed.
1. **Contradiction (0–3s):** both halves of the paradox in one picture.
2. **Apparent evidence (3–12s):** why the naive reading looks right.
3. **The catch (12–25s):** the hidden variable.
4. **Pressure (25–40s):** the paradox tightens; a musical shift.
5. **Revelation (40–53s):** the principle, stated plainly, with a diagram.
6. **Payoff (53–65s):** a reversal that proves the principle, then the lesson.

Episode 001 uses `counterintuitive-truth`.
