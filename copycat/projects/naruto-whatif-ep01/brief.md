Pace target: **~160 spoken words per minute** end to end (tight pauses, no dead air).
Format: full-cast audio drama — a third-person narrator plus distinct character voices, **no
"he said / she said" tags**, short punchy declarative sentences written for TTS, scene-matched
anime stills with slow camera moves. Roughly 50–55% narrator, 45–50% dialogue.

## The single divergence rule

Canon: every shadow clone returns its memories to the user when it disperses. Naruto is not told
until he is sixteen (Kakashi, wind-nature training). The jutsu was invented by the Second Hokage,
Tobirama, for reconnaissance.

**The one change:** on the night he steals the Scroll of Seals, a clone trips and pops, Naruto
feels a scraped knee that is not his, and he reads the line under the hand seals — Lord Second's
warning: *what the clone learns, the caster keeps.*

**The series rule (say it on screen):** *He does not get stronger. He gets to read first.*
Naruto never receives a power he does not have in canon at that point. Every canon arc is solved
by preparation: clones as students, scouts and labour, and the Hokage's archive. Wins come from
paperwork, patience and a thousand pairs of eyes. He stays Naruto: loud, stubborn, lonely, kind.

## Rules of the cheat code (they are stakes, not flavour)

1. The knowing comes back. The muscles do not.
2. The tiredness comes back too, all at once. Fifty clones for an hour = a fifty-hour headache.
   First big test: he blacks out for nineteen hours. After that: batches of ten.
3. A clone that is hit pops — and he keeps the memory of being hit.
4. Every clone carries a share of his chakra — and a share of the fox.
5. A thousand clones watching one moment = that moment remembered from a thousand angles.
   (First felt in Ep1 as Iruka's face; becomes a weapon in Ep3.)

Honest maths: 200 clones × 10 hours = 2,000 hours a day; most genin do not train 2,000 hours a year.

## Signature device: the CANON stamp

When the narrator states something that is literally canon, a rubber stamp "CANON" thunks onto
the frame with a one-line note. Absurd premise, receipts on screen. 6–9 per episode, never on
invented material, never an invented episode number. Beat field:
`"stamp": {"label": "CANON", "note": "<= 60 chars"}`.

Approved canon facts (writers may only stamp from this list; notes may be reworded ≤ 60 chars):
- Shadow clones return their memories — `Kakashi explains it. Naruto is 16.`
- Tobirama invented the Shadow Clone Jutsu — `Created by the Second Hokage for scouting.`
- Naruto learned it in one night — `One night. A few hours.`
- Clone fatigue stacks on the user — `Kakashi's warning, wind training.`
- The Third's crystal ball — `The Third's Telescope Jutsu.`
- Kakashi never passed a team before Team 7 — `Every team before Team 7 failed.`
- Kakashi is always late — `Every. Single. Time.`
- Why Kakashi is late — `The memorial stone. Obito's name.`
- Naruto transformed into a shuriken vs Zabuza — `He does this in the Land of Waves.`
- As Seventh Hokage Naruto runs the office with clones — `Seventh Hokage. Same trick.`
- Kurama was sealed in Kushina before Naruto — `Kushina was the previous host.`
- The Third decreed silence about the Nine-Tails — `The Third's law.`
- Tazuna lied about the mission rank — `Paid for a C-rank. It was not.`
- The puddle on a dry day (Demon Brothers) — `It had not rained in days.`
- A hunter-nin disposes of the body on the spot — `Kakashi realises this too late.`
- Gato planned to betray Zabuza — `Gato never meant to pay.`
- Tora the cat keeps escaping — `The Daimyo's wife's cat. Again.`
- The first Chunin exam tests information gathering — `You were supposed to cheat.`
- Kabuto failed the exam seven times — `Seven attempts. On purpose.`
- Orochimaru killed the Fourth Kazekage and took his place — `The Kazekage was already dead.`
- The Third can use shadow clones — `He uses them against Orochimaru.`
- Kakashi and Sasuke arrive late to the final — `Late to the Chunin final, too.`

## Episode 1 — outline (cold open + 6 chapters)

**COLD OPEN (≤ 35 s).** Hook first: six days after graduating dead last, Naruto Uzumaki sits in
the Hokage's archive with two hundred copies of himself, reading every mission report the Leaf
has filed in twenty years — and the Third Hokage ordered it. Turn: this is not a power-up, it is
one line of text. Stamp (memories return; "Naruto is 16"). "Tonight he is twelve. And he keeps
reading." The series rule: he does not get stronger; he gets to read first. First spoken word
inside 2 seconds.

**CH1 — THE LINE HE SKIPPED.** Forest, night. One perfect clone at last; it trips, pops; "Ow —
wait. I didn't fall." The margin note (narrator reads it; no Tobirama speaker). Stamp. The
experiment: a clone reads the next line behind a tree; pop; he knows it. "I can be in two
classes at once… I can be in ALL of them." Iruka, Mizuki — the canon scene kept SHORT: the fox
revealed in two lines, the shuriken in Iruka's back, the overheard speech ("He's Naruto Uzumaki
of the Hidden Leaf"), a thousand clones. **New beat, the heart of the chapter:** the clones
dispel and a thousand memories come home — Mizuki's fist, the moon, and Iruka crying, a thousand
times. The first person who ever chose him, and he keeps every copy. He is sick on the grass.
Iruka thinks it is chakra exhaustion. Headband.

**CH2 — A THOUSAND TEACHERS.** Next morning on the roof, twenty clones, rules 1–3 as fast comedy
(who has to get punched). The blackout: fifty clones, one hour, all at once — nineteen hours;
wakes at dusk with a crow on his chest. "Okay. Batches of ten." What a kid who slept through the
Academy does with this: clones at the back of every class. The library throws out "that boy" —
so thirty clones return as thirty different old men, all reading the same beginner's
chakra-control primer. Leaf exercise ×200: he learns why his clone jutsu always failed — not too
little chakra, far too much through a pinhole. The maths line. Ichiraku with Iruka: Naruto
answers a history question correctly and Iruka nearly chokes. Give Naruto one or two small,
specific observations born of loneliness (he knows which shop will serve him and at what hour;
he knows people by their footsteps because nobody ever walked toward him). Hiruzen at the
crystal ball. Stamp. He is not angry. He is doing the same arithmetic — and looking at his desk.

**CH3 — THE PUNISHMENT.** Hokage's office. Sentence: one month of filing duty in the archive,
unclassified reports only. "FILING?! I'm a ninja now!" — "Then file like one." A staff form to
sign (plant C). Two hundred Narutos filing: 5–6 quick comic beats (a clone stamps another
clone's forehead; a bucket-chain of scrolls; the Third's in-tray empty for the first time in
thirty years; optional Seventh Hokage stamp). The mail desk: the Kazekage's downstroke (plant A)
— light, funny, memorable. Narrator gives Hiruzen's real reasons: keep the boy where he can see
him; and let a lonely child finally be inside the building.

**CH4 — TWENTY YEARS OF HOMEWORK.** Funny turns serious: these are after-action reports. Every
ambush that worked, every formation that failed, the reports that stop mid-sentence. "Most genin
learn from one teacher. Naruto is learning from every shinobi who came home — and a few who
didn't." He goes quiet for the first time in his life. The puddle report (plant F). He learns to
read what is NOT there: one week in October, twelve years ago, every file is in a different
clerk's handwriting — and the Third has put that shelf off limits. Then a thin folder: Kakashi
Hatake, jonin instructor evaluations — every team FAILED, same reason, same lazy handwriting.
Stamp. Also: eleven reports note he arrived late (plant D). Tomorrow is team assignment.

**CH5 — THE BELL TEST THAT BROKE.** Team 7. Kakashi is three hours late. Stamp. Naruto used the
three hours to prepare the field. He tells Sasuke and Sakura the truth: teamwork test, every
team failed for the same reason — "I filed the paperwork." Sasuke does not believe the dead
last. THE OBSTACLE IS TRUST, NOT POWER. Naruto wins them in character: "Take mine. I'll be the
one who goes back to the Academy. I just need two of us to pass." — which is the answer to the
test, and Sakura hears it. The test, fast and tactical: Kakashi bored with his book; Sasuke's
fireball makes him move; Sakura's kunai cuts a wire strung at dawn; Sasuke throws a windmill
shuriken, Kakashi sidesteps — the shuriken is Naruto. Stamp. A hand closes on a bell. "…Who
taught you that?" — "A lot of me." First team Kakashi has ever passed.

**CH6 — THE DRAWER.** Night. Kakashi in the Hokage's office: "Lord Third. What exactly have you
let him read?" Hiruzen: only what any clerk reads. A beat too long. Cut to the archive, 2 a.m.,
empty — except one filing box that is not a filing box (plant H). The off-limits shelf. A
medical-leave roster, October, twelve years ago. Alphabetical. One line inked out. Above it:
Uzuki. Below it: Yakushi. "Somebody blacked out the name. Nobody thought to re-alphabetise the
page." He has filed the whole clan registry; exactly one name fits in that gap, and he writes it
on every form. Start of leave: counted forward, it ends on October tenth — his birthday.
Authorised by: the Fourth Hokage. The clone pops. Across the village Naruto sits up in bed with
a memory he did not live. "…Uzumaki. There was another one." Under the seal, one enormous eye
opens. KURAMA, his only lines of the episode: "A thousand of you, brat. And every one carries a
piece of me. Did you think I wasn't reading too?" … "Her name was Kushina. I should know. I
lived in her before I lived in you." Stamp (Kushina was the previous host). Narrator: Part Two
tease — "a twelve-year-old asks the Third Hokage one question, and the old man has to decide
whether to lie to a thousand witnesses." Last line is Naruto's, quiet.

## Episode 2 — THE QUESTION (outline)

Cold open: Hiruzen's office is full. Every chair, the windowsill, the corridor — Naruto. One
question: "Who was Kushina Uzumaki?" The Third's law (stamp). Hiruzen's choice; he tells the
truth — mother, father, the night, the masked man he never identified. Naruto does not rage. He
asks for one thing: their mission reports. He meets his father through paperwork (plant J) and
his mother through an incident file of everything she ever broke. Minato's reports on his own
genin team: a boy named Kakashi, a boy named Obito, a girl named Rin — and the dates (payoff D:
the memorial stone, in silence). Inside the seal: Naruto and Kurama on equal footing — not
friends; a trade (plant/payoff I): a clone that reads to the fox; in return, one true memory of
October 10th: a man in a mask. Danzo notices a jinchūriki with archive access; a Root agent
searches the archive under a filing box's eyes (H); Danzo's requisition dies on a staff form
(payoff C). Comedy engine: D-rank missions — forty in one day, Tora the cat caught eleven times
(stamp), the mission desk runs out of D-ranks. He reads the Hidden Mist appendix (plant G).
Cliffhanger: a bridge builder who paid for a C-rank; a puddle on a dry road.

## Episode 3 — THE BRIDGE (outline)

The puddle (payoff F, stamp); the Demon Brothers go down in seconds; Tazuna's lie priced out
loud (stamp). Zabuza: the Water Prison — Naruto has read four reports that describe it, and the
shuriken trick still works because it is HIS trick (stamp). The fake hunter-nin (payoff G,
stamp): Zabuza is alive and they know it, a week early. The week: tree-walking is already done,
so Kakashi teaches the team something else — how to fight inside mist with clone sentries;
three hundred Narutos build the bridge in six days (K) while Inari watches a boy who simply will
not stop. Haku in the woods: the conversation about precious people, kept; this Naruto recognises
the masked boy by his footsteps. Gato Shipping: clones as ledgers (H), the line that says
"disposal — no payment". The bridge: mist, mirrors, Sasuke hurt but alive; payoff E — three
hundred clones dispelled at once, the true mirror called, Naruto goes down for nineteen hours.
He wakes to find how it ended: Kakashi's Lightning Blade stopped a hand's width from Haku
because Zabuza had already read the ledger (stamp: Gato never meant to pay). Zabuza and Haku
walk away alive. The Great Naruto Bridge, finished early. Cliffhanger: back home, a stack of
Chunin exam registration forms to file — and one applicant who has failed seven times.

## Episode 4 — THE SIGNATURE (outline)

Kabuto's cards (L, stamp ×2). The written exam (stamp: you were supposed to cheat) — the clerk
realises the paper does not matter. Scout clones in the Forest of Death, the jutsu doing what
Tobirama built it for; the Grass team seen from two hundred trees; Team 7 never meets
Orochimaru; Anko is told in time. Sasuke, unbitten, watching Naruto pull ahead — and Naruto
answers the way only he would: he lends Sasuke forty sparring partners and every report ever
filed on the Sharingan. Gaara: Naruto has read a hospital incident report, and Kurama has an
opinion about badly made cages (I). The month before the final: a diplomatic letter arrives and
the downstrokes are wrong (payoff A, stamp). Nobody believes a filing clerk about a head of
state — except the old man who hired him. "Why are YOU the one sitting in that chair?" (payoff
B; stamp: the Third can use shadow clones). The final: Kakashi and Sasuke are late (stamp) and
the postponement form is already on the proctor's desk (D). The barrier closes on a clone. The
real Third, Jiraiya and the ANBU are already on the roof. Orochimaru escapes with his arms; the
invasion dies in an afternoon; the Third Hokage lives. Season cliffhanger: filing the aftermath,
Naruto finds what is missing from another shelf — no order for the night the Uchiha died, but a
leave slip for one ANBU captain that night, countersigned by Danzo. He looks up. Sasuke is
reading over his shoulder. "…How long have you been standing there?" — "Long enough."

## Writing standard
Short declarative sentences a TTS voice can act. Specific numbers, objects and names. Scenes play
in dialogue; the narrator connects and observes, never recaps what the picture shows. Each
chapter: at least one laugh, one turn, and a last line worth a pause. No "little did he know",
no purple prose, no canon facts that are not canon. "Believe it" at most once per episode.
Acting cues live only in the `direction` field as [bracket] cues.
