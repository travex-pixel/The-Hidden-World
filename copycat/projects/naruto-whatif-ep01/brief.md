# ONE MORE LINE — a Naruto What-If (series bible + Episode 1 brief)

Project id: `naruto-whatif-ep01` · franchise: `naruto` (see `config/franchises/naruto.json`)

**Episode title:** What If Naruto Read ONE More Line Of The Forbidden Scroll? — Part 1
**Thumbnail text:** HE KEPT READING.
**Target:** 9:00–10:30, 1,450–1,650 spoken words, 110–130 beats, cold open + 4 chapters.

## The premise (absurd, but every step is canon-plausible)

Canon: on graduation night Naruto steals the Scroll of Seals and learns the Multi Shadow
Clone Jutsu in a few hours. Canon also says every shadow clone returns its memories and
experience to the user when it disperses — but Naruto is not told this until he is sixteen
(Kakashi, wind-nature training in Shippuden). The jutsu was invented by the Second Hokage,
Tobirama, for reconnaissance.

**The one change:** that night in the forest, a clone trips and pops, Naruto feels a scraped
knee that is not his, looks back down at the scroll, and reads the line under the hand seals —
Lord Second's warning. *What the clone learns, the caster keeps.* A twelve-year-old with more
chakra than anyone alive has just found the only cheat code in the shinobi world, four years
early. Nothing else about the world changes. Everything after is consequence.

**The absurd engine:** the Third Hokage cannot ignore the theft of a forbidden scroll, so he
sentences Naruto to a month of filing duty in the Hokage's archive — where he can watch him.
Two hundred Narutos doing the Hokage's paperwork is funny. Two hundred Narutos reading twenty
years of mission reports is the best education any genin has ever had. And one clone left
behind after hours, transformed into a filing box, is how a village secret gets out.

**Tone:** a real story, not a power fantasy. Comedy from the absurd logistics (clones disguised
as old men in the library, clones arguing with each other, paperwork), heart from Iruka and
loneliness, intrigue from the files. Naruto stays Naruto: loud, stubborn, hungry for
acknowledgement — just suddenly dangerous because he never gets tired of trying.

## Rules of the cheat code (state them on screen; they make it plausible and create stakes)

1. The knowing comes back. The muscles do not. (Skill, memory, chakra control — yes. Strength — no.)
2. The tiredness comes back too, all at once. Fifty clones for an hour is a fifty-hour headache.
   First big test: he blacks out for nineteen hours. After that he dispels in batches of ten.
3. A clone that is hit pops — and he keeps the memory of being hit.
4. Each clone carries a share of his chakra, which means a share of the fox's too. (Sets up the ending.)

Honest maths the narrator may use: 200 clones × 10 hours = 2,000 hours a day. Most genin do not
train 2,000 hours in a year.

## Signature device: the CANON stamp

When the narrator states something that is literally canon, a rubber-stamp graphic "CANON" thunks
onto the corner of the frame with a one-line source note. It is the series' brand: absurd premise,
receipts on screen. Use 5–6 per episode, never on invented material. Script field on the beat:
`"stamp": {"label": "CANON", "note": "<= 60 chars"}`. Approved stamps for Episode 1 (use these
notes; do not invent episode numbers):

- "The Second Hokage invented the jutsu" — note: `Created by Tobirama Senju for scouting.`

## Episode 1 outline

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:30) — hook first, context second
Flash-forward image, stated flat by the narrator: six days after graduating dead last, Naruto
Uzumaki is sitting in the Hokage's archive with two hundred copies of himself, reading every
mission report the Hidden Leaf has filed in twenty years — and the Third Hokage is the one who
ordered it. Then the turn: this is not a power-up; it is one line of text. Stamp #1 (memories
return; "Naruto is 16"). "Tonight he is twelve. And he keeps reading." Hard cut to title sting.
First spoken line must land inside 2 seconds. No "welcome", no recap.

### CH I — THE LINE HE SKIPPED
- Forest, night. Naruto, filthy and exhausted, one perfect clone at last. They grin at each other.
  The clone trips on a root and pops. Naruto grabs his own knee. "Ow— wait. I didn't fall."
- He looks down. Under the hand seals, in cramped brushwork, a note he was about to roll past.
  Tobirama's warning read by the NARRATOR as the note (or a brief TOBIRAMA-less narration; do not
  add a Tobirama speaker). Stamp #2. "What the clone learns, the caster keeps. For this reason the
  technique is forbidden: the mind fails long before the chakra does."
- The experiment: one clone reads the next line of the scroll around a tree; pop; Naruto knows it.
  The slow grin. "...I can be in two classes at once." beat "I can be in ALL of them."
- Iruka finds him. Mizuki. Keep the canon scene SHORT (viewers know it): the reveal of the fox in
  two lines, Iruka taking the shuriken, Iruka's speech overheard — "He's not the demon fox. He's
  Naruto Uzumaki of the Hidden Leaf." A thousand clones. Mizuki goes down.
- **The new beat (the emotional centre of the chapter):** the clones dispel and a thousand
  memories come home at once — the same moment from a thousand angles. Mizuki's fist. The moon.
  And Iruka's face, crying, a thousand times: the first person who ever chose him, and he gets to
  keep every single copy. He drops to his knees and throws up. Iruka thinks it is chakra exhaustion.
  It is not. Headband scene, brief and warm.

### CH II — A THOUSAND TEACHERS
- Morning after. He should be asleep. He is on the roof with twenty clones, testing rules 1–3
  (play as quick comedy: clones arguing about who has to get punched).
- The blackout: fifty clones, one hour, dispel all at once — nineteen hours unconscious on the
  training field. Wakes up at dusk with a crow on his chest. "Okay. Batches. Batches of ten."
- What does a kid who slept through the Academy do with this? Everything he missed. Clones in the
  back of every Academy classroom. The library throws him out (he is "that boy") — so thirty
  clones walk back in transformed as thirty different old men. One librarian notices that all
  thirty are reading the same beginner's chakra-control primer.
- Leaf-balancing and tree-walking with two hundred clones. He finally understands why his clone
  jutsu always failed: not too little chakra — far too much, poured through a pinhole.
- The maths line (2,000 hours a day).
- Hiruzen, pipe in hand, watching through the crystal ball. Stamp #3. He is not angry. He is
  doing the same arithmetic — and looking at the paperwork on his own desk.

### CH III — THE PUNISHMENT
- Hokage's office. Naruto braced to be yelled at. Hiruzen, dry: stealing the Scroll of Seals is a
  crime; the sentence is one month of filing duty in the Hokage's archive. Unclassified mission
  reports only. Naruto: "FILING?! I'm a ninja now!" Hiruzen: "Then file like one."
  (His real reasons, given by the narrator: keep the boy where he can see him; and keep him busy
  somewhere a lonely kid is finally allowed to be inside the building.)
- The absurd image: two hundred Narutos filing. Comedy montage in 4–5 fast beats (a clone stamps
  another clone's forehead; a chain of clones passing scrolls; Hiruzen's in-tray empty for the
  first time in thirty years; optional Stamp "Seventh Hokage. Same trick.").
- The turn from funny to serious: mission reports are after-action reports. Every ambush that
  worked. Every formation that failed. Every report that ends mid-sentence. "Most genin learn
  from one teacher. Naruto is learning from every shinobi who ever came home — and a few who
  didn't." He goes quiet for the first time in his life.
- He finds a thin folder: Kakashi Hatake, jonin instructor evaluations. Every genin team tested:
  FAILED. Same reason in the same lazy handwriting every time. Stamp #4. And tomorrow is team
  assignments.

### CH IV — THE BELL TEST THAT BROKE
- Team 7 assigned. Kakashi is three hours late. Stamp #5 ("Every. Single. Time."). Naruto knew he
  would be — it is in eleven separate reports — so he has had three hours to prepare the field.
- Naruto tells Sasuke and Sakura the truth flat out: it is a teamwork test, he has failed every
  team for the same reason, "I filed the paperwork." Sasuke does not believe the dead last.
  Knowing the answer is useless if nobody will listen: THE REAL OBSTACLE IS TRUST, not power.
  Naruto wins them with something specific and in-character (he offers Sasuke the bell: "Take
  mine. I'll be the one who goes back to the Academy. I just need two of us to pass." — which is
  the actual answer to the test, and Sakura hears it).
- The test, fast: Kakashi with his book, bored. Sasuke's fireball makes him move. Sakura's kunai
  cuts a wire Naruto strung at dawn. Sasuke throws a windmill shuriken; Kakashi sidesteps it —
  and the shuriken is Naruto. Stamp #6. A hand closes on a bell. Kakashi's one visible eye goes
  wide. "...Who taught you that?" — "A lot of me."
- Kakashi passes them: the first team he has ever passed, in the first ten minutes. That night he
  is in the Hokage's office: "Lord Third. What exactly have you let him read?"
- **Cliffhanger.** The archive, 2 a.m., empty — except one filing box that is not a filing box.
  The clone Naruto left behind finds a drawer sealed with a formula. He has seen that pattern
  every day of his life. It is on his stomach. Inside: one sheet. Medical leave, twelve years ago,
  October. Name: Kushina Uzumaki. Authorised by the Fourth Hokage. The clone pops.
  Across the village Naruto sits up in bed holding a memory he did not live. "...Uzumaki.
  Somebody had my name."
- Under the seal, in the dark, one enormous eye opens. KURAMA (first and only line of the
  episode): "A thousand of you, brat... and every one carries a piece of me. Did you think I
  wasn't reading too?"
- NARRATOR Part Two tease, then last line to Naruto. Tease: "In Part Two, a twelve-year-old asks
  the Third Hokage one question — and the old man has to decide whether to lie to a thousand
  witnesses."
