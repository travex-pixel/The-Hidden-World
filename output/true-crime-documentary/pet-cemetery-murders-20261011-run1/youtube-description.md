St. Bernard Parish, Louisiana, April 1985: Dorothy Thompson, who ran a small pet cemetery, disappeared days before her lawsuit over her land was due in court. Less than a month later her body was found in the Mississippi River, weighted down with steel chains. Twenty-four years later a man told police the groundskeeper had confessed, and said her ghost was haunting his dreams. This is the real case behind HBO's The Pet Cemetery Murders: A Ghost Story, and what the record shows.

LEGAL STATUS: Brandon Nodier was indicted for second-degree murder in April 2012. In 2014, under a plea agreement, he pleaded guilty to MANSLAUGHTER, a reduced charge, and was sentenced to 10 years. He was never convicted of murder. The ghost accounts are what witnesses said, as police and news outlets reported them; this film does not present them as fact. The second witness was not named by police and is not named here.

ILLUSTRATIONS: every person shown is an illustrated representation, not a likeness; witnesses are anonymous silhouettes; the cemetery, river and rooms are generic illustrations, and documents are our own summaries. Dorothy Thompson is not depicted.
