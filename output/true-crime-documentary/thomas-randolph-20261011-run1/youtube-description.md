Las Vegas, May 2008: Thomas Randolph called 911 and said a masked intruder had shot his wife, Sharon. Prosecutors alleged he had hired the man, handyman Michael Miller, to kill her, then shot Miller to get rid of a witness. Two juries convicted him, and the first verdict was thrown out because jurors heard about a death in Utah that he had been acquitted of. This is the true story behind Netflix's The Widower, and where Thomas Randolph is now.

LEGAL STATUS: Thomas Randolph was CONVICTED in August 2023 of conspiracy to commit murder and two counts of murder (Sharon Causse and Michael Miller), sentenced in April 2024 to 60 years to life, and the Nevada Supreme Court affirmed on 31 August 2026. A 2017 conviction and two death sentences in the same case were reversed in December 2020. In Utah he was ACQUITTED in 1989 of murder in the death of Becky Gault, and pleaded guilty to felony witness tampering. No charge was ever brought over the deaths of Francis Randolph or Leona Stapleton. He says he is innocent. Prosecution evidence is attributed to prosecutors or police; the defence's answers are included.
