Las Vegas, 7 September 1996: Tupac Shakur was shot at a red light and died six days later. For 27 years nobody was charged. On 31 August 2026 a jury convicted Duane "Keffe D" Davis of first-degree murder after less than three hours. Prosecutors never said he fired the gun: they said he planned the shooting and supplied the weapon, and the heart of their case was his own words, in a 2008 police interview, on TV and in a memoir. He now says he only told those stories because he was paid. This is how the case reached a verdict, and what the record still can't tell us.

LEGAL STATUS (as of 12 October 2026): Duane Davis was CONVICTED on 31 August 2026 of first-degree murder with use of a deadly weapon. He is due to be sentenced on 13 October 2026 and faces up to life without parole. He says he is innocent and plans to appeal. Everything Davis said in interviews and his memoir is presented as his account. Orlando Anderson, whom Davis named in 2008 as the shooter, denied any involvement, was never charged and died in 1998. Suge Knight survived the shooting and has never been charged in it. No one else has been charged; the district attorney said after the verdict there is not enough evidence to charge anyone else.
