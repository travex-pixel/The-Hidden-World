# Source ledger: *The Murder That Sent the Wrong Man to Prison* (Betsy Faria case)

Research cutoff: **11 October 2026**. Every narrated claim is listed below with its sources and status.

## How this ledger was verified (read this first)

**Limitation.** This production environment's network policy blocks direct page fetches to news and court sites. `WebFetch` returned `ENOTFOUND`, and `curl` was refused by the proxy. So every claim below was checked against **search-engine result excerpts tied to the cited URLs**. No full article or court record was opened.

How claims were handled:
- Each claim that made it into the narration was confirmed independently in at least two search passes.
- Wherever possible, the confirmations came from different outlets.
- Claims that only one weak source supported were cut from the script. Those cuts are listed at the end of this ledger.

**Before publishing, a human reviewer should open each link marked ⚠ and confirm the wording.**

Status tags:
- **ESTABLISHED:** a court outcome or record, or several credible outlets agree.
- **REPORTED:** credible reporting, but not a court finding.
- **ALLEGED:** a party's claim (prosecution or defense) that has not been proven.
- **DISPUTED:** contested between the parties.

## Current legal status (as of the latest reporting found, early October 2026)

- **Russ Faria:** acquitted on 6 Nov 2015. Listed by the National Registry of Exonerations.
- **Pam Hupp, Betsy Faria case:** charged, **not convicted**. She has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent. A bench trial is scheduled for January 2028.
- **Pam Hupp, Louis Gumpenberger case:** convicted by Alford plea; serving life without parole.
- **Shirley Neumann:** manner of death "undetermined"; **no one charged**.
- **Mike Merkel:** perjury charges pending; presumed innocent.

## Claims deliberately left out or softened

- **Defense expert placing Hupp near the house at 7:27 pm.** Only a forum post quoting the Post-Dispatch supports this. The script says only that the phone records "became a key point of dispute."
- **Hupp's trust for the daughters: $100,000 placed in trust, about $99,700 withdrawn, trust revoked.** Search verification failed; omitted.
- **Neumann's insurance amount and Ambien level.** Secondary sources only; omitted.
- **Exact wording of Judge Ohmer's remarks** ("rather disturbing"). Seen only in search excerpts, so it is paraphrased as "criticized the investigation" and attributed to NBC News.
- **The appeals-court sentence in the daughters' case.** Paraphrased, not shown as a quote.
- **Whether Askey has been charged or disciplined.** Not asserted; the script says only that she denies wrongdoing.
- **What Russ told the 911 dispatcher, and any suicide references.** Omitted as unnecessary and sensitive.
- **Low-quality sites** (Grunge, All That's Interesting, forensicfocus forum, forejustice.org) were used only to find leads. None is cited on screen.
