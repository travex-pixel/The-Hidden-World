---
In 2013, a Missouri jury convicted Russ Faria of murdering his wife, Betsy, and he was sentenced to life without parole. Two years later, a judge found him not guilty. Today, prosecutors have charged someone else: the friend who drove Betsy home on the night she died, and who had been made the beneficiary of her $150,000 life insurance policy days earlier.

This documentary traces the case from that night in December 2011 to where it stands in October 2026. It covers the evidence the first jury never heard, the 2016 shooting in O'Fallon, and a murder trial that still has not happened.

⚖️ Pam Hupp has been charged with first-degree murder in Betsy Faria's death and has pleaded not guilty. She has not been convicted in that case and is presumed innocent. She is serving life without parole after an Alford plea in the 2016 death of Louis Gumpenberger. Former investigator Mike Merkel's perjury charges are pending, and he is presumed innocent. Former prosecutor Leah Askey (now Chaney) denies wrongdoing.
