Three days after her father and stepmother were killed, Lizzie Borden stood at the kitchen stove with a dress she said was covered with paint. Ten months later a jury found her not guilty, and no one else was ever charged. So who killed Andrew and Abby Borden?

Told from the trial record: the note no one found, the barn and the pears, the poison story the jury never heard, the grand jury that indicted her only after the dress came out, and the judge appointed by her own lawyer.

LEGAL STATUS: Lizzie Borden was ACQUITTED on 20 June 1893. No one else was ever charged; the killings remain officially unsolved. Witness accounts are attributed to the witness. Theories about others are theories, not findings.

ILLUSTRATIONS: every person shown is an illustrated representation, not a likeness; houses, rooms and documents are generic illustrations or our own summaries. Andrew and Abby Borden are not depicted.
