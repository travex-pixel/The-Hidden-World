Chapel Hill, North Carolina, September 2012: Faith Hedgepeth, a 19-year-old UNC student, was found dead in an off-campus apartment. On the bed near her, police found a note on a fast-food bag that they believed her killer wrote. For nine years no one was arrested. In 2021 police charged Miguel Salguero-Olivares, and investigators said his DNA matched the scene. His trial is now under way in Durham. This is what the jury has heard so far, what it won't hear, and what both sides say.

LEGAL STATUS (as of 13 October 2026): Miguel Salguero-Olivares is charged with first-degree murder, rape, sexual offense and burglary. He has pleaded NOT GUILTY to all charges and is presumed innocent. The trial is ongoing and there is no verdict. Prosecution evidence is attributed to prosecutors or investigators; the defence's answers are included. The State is not seeking the death penalty.
