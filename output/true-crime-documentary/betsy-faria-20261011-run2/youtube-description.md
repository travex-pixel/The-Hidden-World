---
In 2013, a Missouri jury convicted Russ Faria of murdering his wife, Betsy, and he was sentenced to life without parole. Two years later, a judge found him not guilty. Today, prosecutors have charged someone else: the friend who drove Betsy home on the night she died, and who had been made the beneficiary of her $150,000 life insurance policy days earlier.

This documentary traces the case from that night in December 2011 to where it stands in October 2026. It covers the argument the first jury never heard, the 2016 shooting in O'Fallon, and a murder trial that still has not happened.

⚖️ Pam Hupp has been charged with first-degree murder in Betsy Faria's death and has pleaded not guilty. In September 2026 her lawyers asked the court to exclude evidence about the 2016 case from her trial; no ruling had been reported as of October 11, 2026. She has not been convicted in that case and is presumed innocent. She is serving life without parole after an Alford plea in the 2016 death of Louis Gumpenberger. Former investigator Mike Merkel's perjury charges are pending, and he is presumed innocent. Former prosecutor Leah Askey (now Chaney) denies wrongdoing.

## Pinned comment suggestion

> Russ Faria had four alibi witnesses, a receipt, surveillance video and phone records, and a jury still convicted him in 2013. At that trial, the judge did not let the defense argue that someone else could have done it, under Missouri's "direct connection" rule; the defense argues that made the difference. Should courts set a lower bar for presenting alternative-suspect evidence? Please keep it respectful: Pam Hupp has pleaded not guilty in Betsy's death and has not been tried.
