## 3. What the existing MP4 does well (verified in the gate run)

- **No guilt language.** The gate's G8 language rules find no sentence that calls Hupp guilty in Betsy's death, calls Russ guilty after his acquittal, or links anyone to Neumann's death with guilt language. There are 0 CRITICAL G8 findings across the narration, on-screen text, captions, title, thumbnails and description.
- **Status stated plainly.** The closing narration says it: "charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent" (C12, C13). The on-screen status card appears in the cold open.
- **Allegations attributed.** Every allegation in the Gumpenberger matter is attributed to prosecutors (gate G7: 0 unattributed allegations).
- **No authentic-looking fabrications.** There are no fabricated photographs, recordings or documents. People appear only as labelled silhouettes; documents as labelled illustrative summaries; scenes as labelled reconstructions.

## 4. Requires qualified legal review

1. **Ongoing prosecution of Pam Hupp.** A bench trial is set for January 2028 and a defense motion is pending (C13, C61). Publishing commentary on a pending case carries contempt and prejudice considerations as well as defamation risk; these vary by jurisdiction and audience.
2. **The cold open, the Neumann segment, and the title.** See LR-02, LR-04 and LR-01.
3. **Statements about officials.** The original prosecutor (C54) and an investigator facing perjury charges (C53).
4. **Shorts and thumbnails as standalone surfaces.** These are seen without the film's context.
