# Title options

1. What If You Woke Up in 1340 Knowing the Future?
2. You Know the Plague Is Coming. Nobody Believes You
3. Could Modern Knowledge Survive Medieval England?

Recommended: option 1.

## How they were judged

The house packaging rules (`docs/PACKAGING.md`) are tested in `tests/test_history.py`:
* titles must be 28–50 characters;
* no ALL-CAPS words;
* specific nouns early;
* the thumbnail text must not repeat the title;
* every promise must be paid off on screen, early.

| | Title | Chars | Curiosity gap | Specificity | Promise paid off | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What If You Woke Up in 1340 Knowing the Future? | 47 | High: "knowing the future" asks what you'd do | A year, not "medieval times" | 0:00 you wake; 0:17 "in about eight years, the Black Death…" | "What if" titles are common; the year and the thumbnail's eight-year clock set it apart |
| 2 | You Know the Plague Is Coming. Nobody Believes You | 50 | High: emotional, story-first | Plague, a clear conflict | 0:17 the clock; 5:25 the lord laughs | It promises a plague story, but the plague is only one act of six |
| 3 | Could Modern Knowledge Survive Medieval England? | 48 | Medium: reads as an essay question | Broad | Every act | Weaker hook; no stakes or timer |

## Selection
**Option 1.** It is the closest to the brief's working title, but specific (1340, not "medieval Europe").
* **The first 20 seconds deliver it:** the wake-up and the eight-year clock.
* **It pairs with the thumbnail:** thumbnail A shows a phone with no signal, the plague map and "8 YEARS LEFT". The
  text adds the stakes the title leaves out, and repeats none of its words.

Options 2 and 3, with thumbnails B ("THEY LAUGHED") and C ("BAD IDEA"), are ready for YouTube's Test & Compare.
