## Recommendation: #1, "They Quietly Removed Him From the Meeting. Here's What He Did Next."
- The first sentence is a small, specific injustice almost everyone recognises. The second promises a response, not revenge, and the episode delivers one: he asks, and then he holds the door for someone else.
- The first 40 characters ("They Quietly Removed Him From the Meetin…") carry the hook on a phone.
- It pairs with the thumbnail ("LEFT OUT?" and the calendar notice "You are no longer an attendee.") without repeating it.

**A/B test partner:** #2, "Why Being Left Out at Work Hurts More Than You Think". It is stronger for search and calmer in tone.

**Avoid:** #5. It claims proof of intent, and the episode deliberately leaves Graham's motives open.
