## Recommendation: #1, "He Stole Her Idea in the Meeting. He Didn't Even Know."
- The first sentence is the situation millions of people have lived through. The second is the twist, and it is true to the episode: it is a memory error (cryptomnesia), not a lie.
- It fits a mobile feed. The first 40 characters ("He Stole Her Idea in the Meeting. He Di…") carry the hook.
- It pairs with the thumbnail ("WHOSE IDEA?" plus Leah's timestamped message) without repeating it.

**A/B test partner:** #3, "Why People Take Credit for Your Ideas (Without Realising)". It is stronger for search and weaker on emotion.

**Avoid:** #2 and #5. They overstate. Daniel doesn't get the promotion handed to him, and "it's worse" is vague bait.
