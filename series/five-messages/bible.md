# FIVE MESSAGES — Season 1 story bible

**Logline:** The morning after Mara exposes her boss's fraud, an unknown number texts her: *"You didn't win. You were chosen."* Every message that follows predicts exactly what someone will do, and it is always right.

**Why this concept:** a mystery the viewer can play along with (who is the sender?), one concrete prediction per episode that pays off inside the episode, a betrayal by a friend, a twist that recontextualises the villain of the pilot, and a finale where the protagonist turns the manipulator's own method against her. Every episode can be watched alone because the message itself is the hook. It sustains more seasons: the last text comes from a new unknown number.

**Theme:** people are predictable when you know what they want, and flattery is how you find out. The season shows the same move (praise → request → deadline) used on Theo, on Mara, on Priya, and finally refused.

## The true story (what actually happened)
Celeste built Northgate. She flattered Theo into preparing it ("you're brilliant") and arranged for Mara to catch him, so Theo would take the fall and Mara would owe Celeste her promotion. Priya fed Celeste information about Mara in exchange for a promised promotion, believing Celeste when she said she was "protecting" Mara. Theo, who had watched Celeste run the same moves on him, sent the anonymous messages from a second phone, because Mara would never have trusted him directly. Priya recorded her Thursday dinner with Celeste.

## Continuity rules (tested where possible)
1. **The ring:** Celeste's silver signet ring appears in Ep 1 (planted, not explained), Ep 3 (on the shadowed guest, then the flashback), Ep 5 (removed). Same design every time.
2. **9:15:** the offer in Ep 1 and the resignation in Ep 5 both happen at 9:15.
3. **The Linden:** Ep 2 calendar, Ep 3 sign, Ep 5 recording label.
4. **Unknown sender styling:** grey "?" avatar, contact name "Unknown", cold grey bubbles.
5. **Theo:** shirt sleeves, no jacket, all season. Mara's look is unchanged across episodes.
6. **The fair-play clue:** Theo's Ep 2 line "Just ask Priya who she told" repeats Ep 1's anonymous message.

## Psychology notes (no on-screen factual claims)
Flattery and conditional approval, commitment ("say yes" before the offer), and reciprocity (Mara feels she owes Celeste). The episodes dramatise these as character choices; they do not diagnose anyone.
