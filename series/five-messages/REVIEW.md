# Season 1 review (critical, not self-congratulatory)

Based on the rendered MP4s: contact sheets of all five, per-shot review sheets, automated QA and measured audio levels. I could not listen to the audio in this environment.

| Lens | Score /10 | Strengths | Weaknesses to fix next |
|------|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| Retention strategist | 7 | Ep 1, 3, 4 open on a readable phone message in frame 1; each episode pays off its own prediction and ends on a new question; 27–32 s suits Shorts loops; titles match what the video shows. | Ep 2 opens on a medium shot with no text on screen until the first line lands, a weaker first second than the others. Phone-thread shots are near-static; the end card (1–2 s) may cost loop replays. |
| Animation director | 6 | Consistent cast, wardrobe and lighting grades; seven distinct environments; blinking, gaze, expression blends and audio-driven mouths; inserts (ring, calendar, voice memo) add texture. | Body acting is minimal: no hand gestures, walks only as silhouettes, two-shots are static. The three women share one face template and are told apart mainly by hair, skin and wardrobe. Camera moves are gentle pushes; no true cuts within a scene. |
| Script editor | 7 | Clear spine (one message per episode), fair-play clue in Ep 2, the ring planted in Ep 1 and paid off in Ep 3 and Ep 5, the theme mirrored through every character. | Ep 5 resolves fast: the recording, Priya's return and the resignation land in about 12 s. "Well played" is a familiar line. Theo's motive could use one more beat. |
| Software QA | 8 | Each episode: media checks, black-frame scan, caption timing, dialogue level, continuity tests; reproducible, resumable, re-rendered identically in CI. | Lip motion was measured, not inspected by eye at speed; audio quality is unverified by ear; the YouTube upload path is untested against the real API. |
| First-time viewer | 7 | Ep 1 explains itself in 5 s; recap captions help Ep 4 and Ep 5 stand alone; reveals are spaced. | Viewers who skip Ep 1 may not catch why 9:15 matters in Ep 5. |

**Top three improvements for Season 2:** (1) hand and arm gestures in the rig, plus a short walk cycle; (2) a bolder text hook in the first frame of every episode; (3) one extra beat of breathing room in each finale.
