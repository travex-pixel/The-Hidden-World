# Audience-Interest Evidence: Candidate Cases

Research date: 2026-10-11

## Method and limits (read first)

- **How this was checked:** every figure below came from WebSearch result extracts. In this session WebFetch failed on every domain (DNS `ENOTFOUND`, including netflix.com, wikipedia.org, abc.net.au and the .gov.au sites), and direct `curl` was refused by the egress proxy (403). So I could not open the pages themselves. Each URL is the page the search engine quoted for that figure.
- **"Views" (Netflix):** Netflix counts a view as hours viewed divided by runtime. A view count can be weekly (from the Tudum Top 10) or half-yearly (from the "What We Watched" engagement report), and each row says which.
- **Strength ratings:**
  - **strong:** an official platform figure, or a primary record quoted consistently by several outlets.
  - **moderate:** a credible trade or major-outlet report from a single source, or a figure that is qualitative only.
  - **weak:** a third-party tracker such as FlixPatrol or JustWatch, or a single non-major outlet.
  - **unverified:** the sources conflict, or I could not confirm it.
- **YouTube:** I could not verify any YouTube view counts, because the pages could not be loaded. None are claimed here.
