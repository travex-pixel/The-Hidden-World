# Case Files: next slate. US "one clue" cases (7 candidates)

## Method and limits

- **Research date:** 2026-10-11.
- **Only search-result extracts were used.** WebFetch and curl are blocked in this environment. Every claim below comes from WebSearch result summaries of the URL(s) cited next to it. No full page was read, including the FBI, Wikipedia and newspaper pages listed. Quotes are what the search extracts quoted. They have not been checked against the original pages.
- **How citations work here.** The search tool returns a summary of a result set. Sometimes the summary does not say which of several URLs a detail came from. In those cases the claim is cited to the group of URLs it appeared with, and the line says so.
- **Search modes.** "Standard" mode was used for background facts. "Extended" mode was used for anything from 2024–2026 (pardons, parole, new documentaries, sentencing of Bulger's killers, the Madoff Victim Fund, 2026 anniversary programming).
- **The search budget ran out partway through.** The shared limit of 200 WebSearch calls per turn ran out. The last searches (the Ramirez neighbours' names, 2024–2026 Ramirez media, the La Opinión front page, and 2024–2026 Lindbergh media) were refused. So:
  - **NOT CHECKED in this pass:** 2024–2026 documentaries and podcasts for Lindbergh, Ramirez and Madoff; the exact dates of Harry Markopolos's SEC submissions; the exact days of the 1976–77 Son of Sam attacks (only the months were confirmed); Madoff's plea and sentencing dates; the names of the East LA residents who held Ramirez; family objections to media coverage for Lindbergh, Ramirez and Madoff.
  - Lines marked **PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked)** come from background knowledge and were not confirmed by a search this session. Treat them as a checklist, not as sources. The user can send a follow-up message to continue the searches.
- **Labels:**
  - **VERIFIED** means at least two independent reputable sources (official or court records, FBI/DOJ, AP/Reuters, major newspapers or broadcasters, museums) appeared in the extracts.
  - **SINGLE SOURCE** means only one reputable source.
  - **UNVERIFIED** means only weak sources, or sources that conflict.
  - **MYTH** means reputable sources contradict the popular version.
  - Wikipedia, content farms and aggregators are used only as pointers or as corroboration, never as one of the two sources.
- apnews.com could not be used as a search filter. AP material appears here only where outlets syndicated it (for example AP stories on station sites).

## 1. Whitey Bulger: the stray cat and the daytime-TV ad (2011)

**Hook:** For 16 years the FBI hunted the gangster and failed. Then it ran daytime-TV ads about his girlfriend, and a former neighbour who knew her from feeding a stray cat picked up the phone.

### Verified turning point

- **The campaign. VERIFIED.**
  - On **20 June 2011** FBI Boston announced a publicity campaign built around **Catherine Greig**, Bulger's long-time girlfriend. The FBI noted she was not implicated in the murders but was wanted for harbouring him.
  - A **30-second PSA** ran during **daytime shows with many female viewers**, in about **350 time slots across 14 media markets in 10 states**. It was aimed at people who might know her: a neighbour, a hair stylist, a dentist.
  - One report named *The View* and *The Dr. Oz Show* as examples. Coverage also mentions billboards.
  - Sources: [FBI press release, 20 June 2011](https://www.fbi.gov/boston/press-releases/2011/fbi-announces-a-unique-publicity-campaign-to-locate-a-top-ten-fugitive/); [WBUR](https://www.wbur.org/news/2011/06/20/fbi-bulger-girlfriend); [Fox News/AP](https://www.foxnews.com/us/tip-from-publicity-campaign-credited-in-capture-of-most-wanted-mob-boss-james-whitey-bulger). The show names came from this result group without a clear per-URL attribution.
- **The tip. VERIFIED.**
  - A tip reached the FBI's Los Angeles field office on the evening of **21 June**.
  - The Boston special agent in charge called it a "direct result" of the campaign.
  - Sources: [Boston Globe archive](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2011/06/24/archive-whiteybulger-captured/q28y9NixQqNe9VhCcIAt1K/story.html); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna43504992); [FBI story, 23 June 2011](https://www.fbi.gov/news/stories/top-ten-fugitive-james-whitey-bulger-arrested).
- **The tipster and the cat: widely reported, from one original source (the Boston Globe, October 2011).**
  - She was **Anna Björnsdóttir**, Miss Iceland 1974, a former actress and model.
  - She lived near Bulger and Greig in Santa Monica, where they posed as "Charlie and Carol Gasko". She got to know them because Greig fed a stray cat; one extract names the cat "Tiger".
  - She was **back in Iceland** when she saw a **CNN report** about the search, and she called the FBI from Iceland on 21 June.
  - The FBI said it paid **$2.1 million** in rewards. She reportedly received about $2 million.
  - Sources: [PRI](https://www.pri.org/stories/2011-10-10/former-miss-iceland-tipped-fbi-whitey-bulger); [CBS Boston](https://cbsnews.com/boston/news/report-whitey-bulger-tipster-was-former-miss-iceland); [Time NewsFeed](https://newsfeed.time.com/?p=100228); [NBC Los Angeles](https://nbclosangeles.com/news/local/thats-cold-ex-miss-iceland-collecting-2m-for-turning-in-whitey-bulger/1909919); [Santa Monica Daily Press](https://www.smdp.com/whitey-bulger-tipster-revealed); [Boulder Weekly (the $2.1M figure)](https://archives.boulderweekly.com/?p=6533).
  - The FBI itself never publicly confirmed who the tipster was. It had promised confidentiality.
- **The arrest, 22 June 2011. VERIFIED.**
  - Special Agent Scott Garriola had the building manager tell Bulger that his **storage locker** had been broken into. Bulger came down to the garage, where agents arrested him.
  - He first said he was Charles Gasko, then: "You know who I am… I'm Whitey Bulger." (agent's testimony as quoted in the extract)
  - Agents found about **$822,000 in cash**, **30 guns** and false IDs. Early reports said "about $800,000".
  - Sources: [Santa Monica Daily Press, trial coverage](https://www.smdp.com/fbi-agent-details-bulger-arrest-in-santa-monica/); [CBS 60 Minutes](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/60-minutes-capturing-whitey-bulger-2/); [CBS Boston](https://cbsnews.com/boston/news/weapons-ammunition-cash-found-in-whitey-bulgers-apartment).
  - The couple had lived in a rent-controlled apartment there for about 15 years and paid the rent in cash: [WBUR](https://www.wbur.org/news/2011/06/23/bulger-apartment); [The Daily Record](https://thedailyrecord.com/2011/06/23/bulger-lived-in-california-apartment-for-15-years/).

### Key dates

- **Late 1994/early 1995:** Bulger flees after FBI agent **John Connolly** warns him of an indictment. Connolly was convicted of racketeering in May 2002 ([CBS](https://cbsnews.com/news/whitey-bulger-arrest-may-revive-old-scandals); [Fox News/AP](https://www.foxnews.com/us/capture-of-boston-gangster-could-mean-more-scandal.amp)).
- **1999:** Bulger is put on the FBI Top Ten list ([FBI release](https://www.fbi.gov/boston/press-releases/2011/fbi-announces-a-unique-publicity-campaign-to-locate-a-top-ten-fugitive/)).
- **20 / 21 / 22 June 2011:** campaign launched, tip received, arrest.
- **October 2011:** the Boston Globe names the tipster.
- **August 2013:** convicted on 31 of 32 counts, including 11 of the 19 murders charged ([VOA/Reuters](https://www.voanews.com/a/reu-mobster-whitey-bulger-life-sentence-unfathomable-crimes/1790660.html)). The exact day (12 August) is PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked).
- **14 November 2013:** two consecutive life terms plus five years, and $19.5M restitution, from Judge Denise Casper ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/whitey-bulger-gets-two-life-sentences-plus-five-years-flna2d11594929); [WGBH](https://wgbh.org/news/local/2013-11-14/james-whitey-bulger-sentenced-to-2-life-sentences-plus-5-years)).
- **30 October 2018:** Bulger, 89, is beaten to death at USP Hazelton, West Virginia, hours after a transfer ([CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/inmate-sentenced-prison-killing-boston-gangster-james-whitey-bulger/); [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/former-hitman-sentenced-to-25-years-for-prison-slaying-of-crime-boss-whitey-bulger)).

### Legal status (as of 2026-10-11)

- **Bulger:** convicted, then killed in prison in 2018.
- **His killers (2024 sentences):**
  - Fotios "Freddy" Geas pleaded guilty and got 25 years on top of his existing life term.
  - Paul DeCologero got a little over 4 years for assault (the lookout).
  - Sean McKinnon got time served for lying to agents.
  - Sources: [NBC Boston](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/an-ex-mafia-hitman-is-set-for-sentencing-in-the-prison-killing-of-gangster-james-whitey-bulger/3480404/); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/james-whitey-bulger-killing-fotios-geas-sentenced-1950110); [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/inmate-sentenced-prison-killing-boston-gangster-james-whitey-bulger/).
- **Catherine Greig:**
  - Sentenced to 8 years in 2012 for helping Bulger evade capture.
  - Given 21 more months in 2016 for refusing to testify.
  - Finished her sentence in July 2020 and lives in South Boston.
  - Sources: [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2020/07/23/metro/whitey-bulgers-girlfriend-catherine-greig-officially-ends-prison-sentence-back-home-southie); [Bangor Daily News](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2020/07/24/news/whitey-bulgers-girlfriend-completes-prison-sentence/); [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/2667165/whitey-bulgers-girlfriend-gains-more-jail-time-for-refusing-to-testify/).

### People and sensitivities

- **Victims' families.** Bulger was convicted of 11 murders. At sentencing, families were split on whether it brought closure. Tommy Donahue, whose father Michael was killed, called it "the first day that we can finally get on the road for closure" ([CBS Boston](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/families-of-bulger-victims-split-on-whether-sentence-brings-closure); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/whitey-bulger-gets-two-life-sentences-plus-five-years-flna2d11594929)). The extracts showed no objection from families to media treatment, but this was not exhaustively checked.
- **The FBI's own role.** Bulger was an FBI informant protected by a corrupt agent (Connolly). An "FBI genius" framing would be one-sided. A single line acknowledging the tip-off keeps the episode honest.
- **The tipster's privacy.** The FBI had promised confidentiality. Some readers and the Boston Herald criticised the Globe for naming her ([LAist](https://laist.com/news/privacy-shmivacy-the-boston-glob); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna44840737)). Bulger reportedly knew who had tipped him off ([Yahoo News](https://news.yahoo.com/whitey-bulger-knew-former-miss-iceland-ratted-him-163114545.html)). **Recommendation:** say "a former neighbour". Do not name or draw her.
- **Greig** is living and has served her sentence. Avoid mocking or "gangster's moll" framing.
- **Reward dispute.** A Las Vegas man, Keith Messina, sued, saying he had reported a sighting in 2008 ([CBS Boston](https://cbsnews.com/boston/news/man-suing-fbi-after-another-bulger-tipster-was-rewarded-2-million)). Do not claim this was "the only tip ever".

### Myths and exaggerations to avoid

- **"A woman in Iceland recognised her."** MISLEADING. She was a former Santa Monica neighbour who happened to be in Iceland ([PRI](https://www.pri.org/stories/2011-10-10/former-miss-iceland-tipped-fbi-whitey-bulger); [CBS Boston](https://cbsnews.com/boston/news/report-whitey-bulger-tipster-was-former-miss-iceland)).
- **"She saw the FBI's TV ad."** UNVERIFIED. Reports say she saw a CNN report. NBC Los Angeles says the spots ran in 14 cities including San Francisco and San Diego, but not Los Angeles (SINGLE SOURCE: [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/reward-paid-to-whitey-bulger-tipster/2103143)). Safe wording: "the campaign made the news, and a former neighbour saw it."
- **The PSA said Greig had plastic surgery or monthly dental cleanings.** UNVERIFIED. The FBI release extract confirms only that she was likely to have well-kept teeth (a former dental hygienist).
- **"$800,000 hidden in the walls."** Partly wrong. About $822K cash was found. One report says most guns were in holes cut into the walls. Do not say the cash was in the walls.
- **The building name "Princess Eugenia."** UNVERIFIED (not in the extracts).

### Audience signals

- **Hulu, *Hunting Whitey Bulger* (working title).**
  - A three-part docuseries narrated by Ben Affleck, directed by Zackary Canepari, from the Casey Sherman / Dave Wedge book. It was announced in late May 2026.
  - **No premiere date has been announced.** It could land in or near the window.
  - Sources: [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/05/ben-affleck-whitey-bulger-docuseries-hulu-1236918002/); [What's On Disney Plus](https://whatsondisneyplus.com/hulu-developing-new-hunting-whitey-bulger-series-with-ben-affleck/); [ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.net/tv/news/2136955-ben-affleck-hulu-upcoming-crime-tv-show).
- **Older titles:** CBS *FBI True* "Hunting Whitey Bulger" (2023) ([IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt26937456/)), and Joe Berlinger's *Whitey* (2014). *Black Mass* (2015) is PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked).
- **Anniversaries, Oct 2026–Mar 2027:** only the 8th anniversary of his prison killing (30 Oct 2026), which is not a good hook. No round anniversaries.

### Visual potential (2D, non-violent)

- A sun-bleached Santa Monica apartment block near the beach, with palm trees.
- An elderly woman putting down a food bowl for a tabby stray on a walkway.
- A daytime talk show on a living-room TV with an FBI "Have you seen this woman?" card.
- A CNN broadcast on a TV in a snowy Reykjavik flat, then a phone call across the Atlantic.

### Risk: **Low–medium**

## 2. Lindbergh kidnapping: the plate number on the $10 bill (1932–1936)

**Hook:** The "crime of the century" went unsolved for two and a half years. Then a gas-station manager scribbled a licence plate on a $10 bill because he thought it might be fake.

### Verified turning point

- **Why the bill was traceable. VERIFIED (FBI).**
  - The **$50,000 ransom** was paid largely in **gold certificates**.
  - Serial-number lists were sent to banks and businesses in early 1934.
  - The 1933 presidential proclamation recalling gold and gold certificates made the notes stand out.
  - Sources: [FBI, Famous Cases](https://www.fbi.gov/history/famous-cases/lindbergh-kidnapping); [FBI archives, "FBI 100"](https://archives.fbi.gov/archives/news/stories/2008/march/lindbergh_030308).
- **The gas station. VERIFIED.**
  - On **15 September 1934** a customer paid for gas with a **$10 gold certificate** at a station at **127th Street and Lexington Avenue**, Manhattan.
  - Station manager **Walter Lyle** had been warned about counterfeit gold certificates. He wrote the car's New York plate number in the bill's margin.
  - Sources: [History.com (Christopher Klein)](https://www.history.com/articles/the-capture-of-the-charles-lindbergh-baby-kidnapper); [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/history/famous-cases/lindbergh-kidnapping); [Crime+Investigation UK](https://crimeandinvestigation.co.uk/crime-files/lindbergh-kidnapping/arrest).
- **The bank. VERIFIED (FBI).**
  - On **18 September 1934** a teller at the **Corn Exchange Bank and Trust Company** branch at 125th Street and Park Avenue matched the serial number and reported it to the FBI's New York office.
  - Source: [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/history/famous-cases/lindbergh-kidnapping); the History.com extract gives the same sequence.
- **The arrest. VERIFIED.**
  - The plate was traced to **Bruno Richard Hauptmann**, a German-born carpenter living in the Bronx. He was arrested on **19 September 1934** with another ransom bill on him.
  - More than **$13,000** in ransom certificates was later found hidden in his garage.
  - FBI Laboratory handwriting comparison linked him to the ransom notes.
  - Sources: [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/history/famous-cases/lindbergh-kidnapping); [FBI archives, "Lab's first major case"](https://archives.fbi.gov/archives/news/stories/2007/november/fbi-labs-first-major-case-lindbergh-kidnapping); [Crime+Investigation UK](https://www.crimeandinvestigation.co.uk/crime-files/lindbergh-kidnapping).
- **Conflicting details:**
  - Trial testimony quoted in one extract has attendant **John J. Lyons** taking the bill, with Lyle writing the plate. Lyons later received the bill in his pay and took it to the bank. This came from the result set including [Crime Library](https://crimelibrary.org/notorious_murders/famous/lindbergh/5b.html) and [History.com](https://www.history.com/news/the-capture-of-the-charles-lindbergh-baby-kidnapper).
  - Sources disagree on the car (a dark blue Dodge in one, a brown Plymouth in another). **Do not show a specific make or colour.**

### Key dates

- **Night of 1 March 1932:** 20-month-old Charles Lindbergh Jr. is taken from the family home near Hopewell, New Jersey. PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked). The date is standard but was not re-confirmed this session.
- **Spring 1932:** ransom paid; the child's body found in May 1932. PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked).
- **15 / 18 / 19 September 1934:** gas station, bank, arrest (verified above).
- **January–February 1935:** trial in Flemington, NJ, ending in conviction. The verdict date (13 February 1935) is PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked).
- **3 April 1936:** Hauptmann is executed in the electric chair at Trenton State Prison ([Wikipedia: Hauptmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Richard_Hauptmann); [Crime+Investigation UK, trial](https://www.crimeandinvestigation.co.uk/crime-files/lindbergh-kidnapping/trial)).

### Legal status

- Hauptmann was convicted and executed in 1936. His conviction was upheld on appeal, and the US Supreme Court declined to hear the case.
- Governor Harold Hoffman granted a stay in January 1936.
- Hauptmann refused a last-minute offer of commutation that required a confession.
- **No formal exoneration was found.**
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Richard_Hauptmann); [Crime+Investigation UK](https://www.crimeandinvestigation.co.uk/crime-files/lindbergh-kidnapping/trial).

### Lingering doubts (must be acknowledged on screen)

- **Hauptmann's widow.** Anna Hauptmann maintained his innocence until her death in 1994 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Richard_Hauptmann); [Crime Library, theories](https://crimelibrary.org/notorious_murders/famous/lindbergh/theories_8.html)).
- **Critics' points:**
  - The case rested on circumstantial evidence.
  - The chain of custody for the ladder wood was questionable.
  - One eyewitness was described as almost legally blind.
  - Police conduct has been widely criticised.
  - Source: same result set.
- **The counterview.** Crime Library also argues the circumstantial evidence is "plentiful, convincing, and unaltered".
- **Safe script line:** "Hauptmann was convicted and executed, insisting to the end he was innocent. Some historians still doubt he acted alone, or at all."

### People and sensitivities

- The victim was a toddler. **Do not depict the child, the nursery or the body.**
- Descendants of the Lindbergh and Hauptmann families may be living. The extracts showed no family objections to media treatment, but this was NOT CHECKED in depth.
- Charles Lindbergh's later political views can derail comment sections. PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked). Keep the episode on the money trail.
- The possible wrongful execution is a real ethical issue. Do not present the bill as "proof of guilt".

### Myths and exaggerations to avoid

- **"The attendant suspected he was the kidnapper."** MYTH. The plate was noted because of worries about counterfeit gold certificates ([History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/the-capture-of-the-charles-lindbergh-baby-kidnapper)).
- **"One bill convicted him."** Overstated. The bill led police to him. The conviction relied on the garage money, the handwriting and the ladder evidence ([FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/history/famous-cases/lindbergh-kidnapping)).
- **"Hauptmann was later exonerated."** MYTH. There was no formal exoneration.
- **"Hauptmann was proven innocent / proven guilty."** Both overclaim.
- **"The FBI solved it alone."** Be careful. The FBI pages describe its role. NJ State Police and NYPD involvement is PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked). Say "investigators".

### Audience signals

- **2024–2026 documentaries and podcasts:** NOT CHECKED (search budget ran out).
- **Anniversary in the window:** **1 March 2027 is the 95th anniversary of the kidnapping.** The date is standard but was not re-checked this session.
- An evergreen "crime of the century" case. The FBI lists it among its famous cases and its Laboratory's first major case ([FBI archives](https://archives.fbi.gov/archives/news/stories/2007/november/fbi-labs-first-major-case-lindbergh-kidnapping)).

### Visual potential (2D, non-violent)

- A 1934 Harlem gas station with a glass-topped pump, an attendant in a cap, and a pencil writing a plate number in the margin of a golden-hued $10 note.
- A bank teller running a finger down a printed serial-number list.
- A Bronx garage with banknotes hidden behind boards.
- The homemade wooden ladder as an object.
- 1930s "Crime of the Century" newspaper headlines.
- The Flemington courthouse.
- No violence.

### Risk: **Medium**

The victim was a child and the guilt is contested. Living-victim sensitivity is low because of the case's age.

### Verdict: **RECOMMEND**, with the doubts line

## 3. Son of Sam: the parking ticket (1977)

**Hook:** For a year a gunman terrified New York. He was caught because he parked too close to a fire hydrant.

### Verified turning point

- **The last shooting.** On **31 July 1977** in Brooklyn, Stacy Moskowitz was killed. Robert Violante lost an eye and was left legally blind in the other ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/son-of-sam-victims-in-their-own-words/)).
- **The witness. VERIFIED.**
  - A local woman walking her dog saw police **ticketing a cream-coloured car parked by a fire hydrant** about a block from the scene, and a man acting strangely.
  - Her name is spelled Cacilia, Cecilia or Cecelia Davis in different sources.
  - Sources (result group, without per-URL attribution): [Time](https://redesign.time.com/3979004/son-of-sam-caught); [Biography.com](https://www.biography.com/crime-figure/david-berkowitz); [AETV](https://www.aetv.com/articles/david-berkowitz).
- **The ticket. VERIFIED.**
  - Detectives went through the tickets written near the scene that night. One was for a **1970 Ford Galaxie registered to David Berkowitz of Yonkers**.
  - Detective **Edward Zigo** is credited with acting on it.
  - Sources: [History.com](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/august-10/son-of-sam-arrested); [Time](https://redesign.time.com/3979004/son-of-sam-caught); [Biography.com](https://www.biography.com/crime-figure/david-berkowitz); [Yahoo News, "A Parking Ticket Led to His Capture"](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/son-sam-struck-fear-york-202200287.html).
  - A 1977 AP report called it a **$35 ticket** for parking too close to a hydrant. The result set included 1977 newspaper archive pages ([Georgia Historic Newspapers, 11 Aug 1977](https://gahistoricnewspapers.galileo.usg.edu/lccn/sn83009936/1977-08-11/ed-1/seq-1/); [TTU archive](https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/bitstreams/70dee3da-8ad6-41fb-93cc-4ede0d5b474d/download)), but the extract did not say which one carried the figure.

## 4. Bernie Madoff: the confession to his sons, and the warnings the SEC ignored (2008)

**Hook:** The biggest Ponzi scheme in history wasn't cracked by investigators. It ended when Madoff confessed to his sons and they turned him in, years after a whistleblower had handed regulators the maths.

## 5. Oklahoma City bombing: the missing licence plate and the axle (1995)

**Hook:** Less than 90 minutes after the deadliest domestic terror attack in US history, a trooper pulled over a car with no licence plate. Two days later, the FBI found its suspect was already in jail.

### Verified turning point (two threads that converged)

1. **The traffic stop. VERIFIED.**
   - The bomb exploded at **9:02 a.m. on 19 April 1995**.
   - At **10:20 a.m.**, Oklahoma Highway Patrol Trooper **Charles "Charlie" Hanger** stopped a yellow **1977 Mercury Marquis with no rear plate** on I-35 near Perry, Noble County.
   - He found a concealed, loaded gun and arrested McVeigh on traffic and weapons misdemeanour charges. McVeigh was booked into the Noble County jail.
   - Sources: [famous-trials.com timeline (UMKC)](https://famous-trials.com/oklacity/714-mcveigharrest); [FBI, Oklahoma City Bombing](https://stories.fbi.gov/oklahoma-bombing/); [FBI video, case agent Jim Norman](https://www.fbi.gov/video-repository/newss-jim-norman-case-agent-oklahoma-city-bombing-investigation/view); [NLEOMF](https://nleomf.org/?p=1224); [Blackwell Journal-Tribune](https://www.blackwelljournaltribune.net/articles/12597/view/was-timothy-mcveigh-s-arrest-aided-by-divine-intervention).
   - **Timing:** the FBI says "90 minutes after the bombing". The timeline gives about **78 minutes**, and NLEOMF says "seventy minutes". **Use "less than 90 minutes".**
2. **The axle and the VIN. VERIFIED.**
   - The **rear axle housing** of the Ryder truck carried a **partial VIN**.
   - Oklahoma City police technician **Mike McPherson** copied it onto a spare business card. It was traced through the **National Insurance Crime Bureau** to a Ryder truck rented from **Elliott's Body Shop, Junction City, Kansas**.
   - Sources: [Oklahoma City National Memorial & Museum, artifact page](https://memorialmuseum.com/artifact/rear-axle-housing-with-partial-vin/); [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/history/cases-and-criminals/oklahoma-city-bombing); [FBI artifact page](https://www.fbi.gov/history/artifacts/special-agent-barry-blacks-hard-hat). The FBI material associates Special Agent Barry Black with the axle, so accounts differ on who found and recorded it.
   - At trial, a Ryder executive testified that the truck was rented on **17 April 1995** by a customer calling himself "**Bob Kling**", which prosecutors said was McVeigh's alias. This came from a 1997 AP report in the result set; one candidate is the [University Daily Kansan archive](https://digital.lib.ku.edu/islandora/object/ku-udk%3A64005/datastream/OCR/download), but the extract did not name the URL.
   - Another account gives 15 April. That may be the reservation date; UNVERIFIED.
3. **The sketch, the motel and the jail. VERIFIED.**
   - Body-shop staff helped make a composite sketch.
   - Staff at the **Dreamland Motel**, Junction City, recognised it as a guest registered as **Tim McVeigh** (14–18 April).
   - A records check showed a Timothy McVeigh **already in custody in the Noble County jail**. On **21 April** the FBI asked for him to be held, shortly before he was due to be released.
   - Sources: [FBI](https://stories.fbi.gov/oklahoma-bombing/); [1997 defense filing, FAS](https://irp.fas.org/threat/mcveigh/part03.htm); [Crime Library](https://crimelibrary.org/serial_killers/notorious/mcveigh/snag_2.html).
   - **How close he came to release ("hours" or "minutes") is UNVERIFIED.**

### Conflicting details

- **When the axle was found:** the day of the bombing (1997 AP) or 20 April (FBI and later accounts).
- **Distance north of Oklahoma City:** "about 80 miles" on one FBI page, about 60 in an FBI podcast, 62 in Jim Norman's account ([FBI podcast](https://www.fbi.gov/audio-repository/news-podcasts-inside-okbomb.mp3/view)).

## 7. Richard Ramirez, "Night Stalker": his own face on the front page (1985)

**Hook:** After a summer of terror, the Night Stalker walked into an East LA store, saw his own face on the front pages and ran. The neighbourhood caught him.

### Verified turning point

- **The clue behind the photo.**
  - A partial fingerprint from a **stolen Toyota**, found after a witness noted its plate, was matched to Ramirez in a **new computerised fingerprint database**.
  - Police named him and released his photo at a news conference on **30 August 1985** ([Crime+Investigation UK](https://www.crimeandinvestigation.co.uk/articles/how-public-helped-police-catch-night-stalker); [History.com](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/august-31/los-angeles-mob-attacks-night-stalker)).
  - The system name "**Cal-ID**" is **UNVERIFIED** in this pass. Accounts also differ on how the search was run.
- **The recognition and capture, 31 August 1985. VERIFIED.**
  - Ramirez entered an East LA liquor store and saw his face on the front pages ([AETV](https://www.aetv.com/articles/richard-ramirez-capture)).
  - Recognised from a photo shown on TV and in newspapers, he fled and tried to steal a woman's car ([History.com](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/august-31/los-angeles-mob-attacks-night-stalker); [Fox 13/AP](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/night-stalker-richard-ramirez-dead)).
  - Residents of the **3700 block of East Hubbard Street** chased him, struck him (one account says with a fence post) and held him until police arrived.
  - Sources: [NBC Los Angeles](https://nbclosangeles.com/news/richard-ramirez-nightstalker-captured-34-years-ago/77578); [The LA Local](https://thelalocal.org/neighborhoods/boyle-heights/participant-remembers-night-stalker-richard-ramirez-capture-in-east-la/); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/thirty-years-ago-the-night-stalker-was-captured-by-an-angry-mob-1727750592). One extract from this result group says the front page was **La Opinión**.
  - Six neighbours were later honoured by Los Angeles County; Supervisor Kenneth Hahn called them heroes ([UPI archive, 3 Sept 1985](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1985/09/03/Six-neighbors-who-captured-the-suspected-Night-Stalker-as/7322494568000/)).

### Key dates

- **1984–1985:** the attacks. Exact range NOT CHECKED.
- **30 August 1985:** name and photo released.
- **31 August 1985:** capture.
- **1989:** convicted of 13 murders, 5 attempted murders, 11 sexual assaults and 14 burglaries. **7 November 1989:** sentenced to death ([Biography.com](https://www.biography.com/crime-figure/richard-ramirez)).
- **2006:** California Supreme Court rejects his appeal.
- **7 June 2013:** dies aged 53 of complications of B-cell lymphoma at Marin General Hospital, while still on death row ([Biography.com](https://www.biography.com/crime-figure/richard-ramirez); [AETV](https://www.aetv.com/real-crime/richard-ramirez-death)).

### Legal status

Ramirez was convicted and sentenced to death. He died in 2013 before he could be executed.

### People and sensitivities

- **Fan culture.** A persistent fan and "groupie" culture and satanic iconography create a glamorisation risk on short-form platforms. This is PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (not checked) but widely noted.
- **The capture was violent** (vigilante blows). It is hard to show honestly without depicting violence.

- **"Cal-ID matched the print."** UNVERIFIED (the name does not appear in the extracts).

- **"They recognised him by his eyes."** A single quoted claim ([Fox 13](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/night-stalker-richard-ramirez-dead)).
