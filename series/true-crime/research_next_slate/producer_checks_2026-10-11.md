## Agatha Christie's disappearance: 100th anniversary on 3 Dec 2026
- She left her home in Sunningdale on the evening of **3 Dec 1926**, and her car was found the next morning near Newlands Corner, Surrey.
- More than 1,000 police officers and an estimated 15,000 volunteers searched for her.
- She was recognised at the Swan Hydro hotel in Harrogate on **14 Dec 1926**, 11 days later, where she had registered under the surname of her husband's mistress.
- She said she had no memory of those days, and the reason has never been settled. This was not a crime, so the risk for us is low.
- Sources: National Archives blog https://blog.nationalarchives.gov.uk/20speople-investigating-the-strange-disappearance-of-mrs-agatha-christie/ ; History Hit https://www.historyhit.com/the-enduring-mystery-of-agatha-christies-disappearance/ ; Countryfile https://www.countryfile.com/famous-people/agatha-christie-disappearance

## Alexander Litvinenko: 20 years
- Poisoned with polonium-210 in London on **1 Nov 2006**. He died on 23 Nov 2006.
- The UK public inquiry (2016) concluded that Andrei Lugovoi and Dmitry Kovtun carried out the killing, probably on the instructions of the FSB, and that it was "probably approved" by President Putin. Polonium traces were found at many places the two men visited. Both deny involvement, and Russia has refused extradition.
- Attribute every finding to the inquiry.

## Kim Jong-nam: 10th anniversary on 13 Feb 2027
- Killed with VX nerve agent at Kuala Lumpur airport in 2017.
- The two women charged said they believed they were taking part in a TV prank.
  - **Siti Aisyah:** the charge was withdrawn on 11 Mar 2019 (a discharge, not an acquittal).
  - **Doan Thi Huong:** pleaded guilty to a lesser charge of "causing injury" and was released early.
- Four North Koreans named as co-conspirators left Malaysia. Malaysia never formally accused North Korea.

## Lizzie Borden: Netflix *Monster* season 4
- The season, *Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story*, premiered on **17 Sep 2026**. The audience dossier records its Tudum figures.
- She was acquitted in June 1893 after a short deliberation. Her inquest testimony was ruled inadmissible.
- The family friend Alice Russell testified that Lizzie burned a dress in the kitchen stove days after the killings. Lizzie said it was stained with paint.
- No murder weapon was conclusively tied to her. The killings are officially unsolved.
- The drama fictionalises motives and relationships, so script only from the historical record.
- Sources: Woman & Home https://www.womanandhome.com/life/news-entertainment/lizzie-borden-why-was-she-acquitted/ ; Biography https://www.biography.com/movies-tv/a73346578/monster-season-4-the-lizzie-borden-story ; Grazia https://graziadaily.co.uk/life/tv-and-film/monsters-lizzie-borden-true-story/

## Elizabeth Smart: turning point
- In **October 2002** her younger sister Mary Katherine remembered "Emmanuel", a handyman who had worked at the house the year before. She recognised his voice as the abductor's.
- Police made a composite sketch but advised the family not to release it until **February 2003**. After it aired on *America's Most Wanted*, people in Sandy, Utah called police, and Elizabeth was found alive on **12 Mar 2003**.
- Accounts differ on whether the callers recognised him from the sketch or from photos on the news.
- Sources: KSL timeline https://www.ksl.com/article/8139474 ; A&E https://www.aetv.com/articles/who-was-elizabeth-smart-and-how-was-she-kidnapped ; Washington Times 2003-03-12 https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2003/mar/12/20030312-085915-8532r/
