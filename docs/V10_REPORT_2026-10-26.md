# THE HIDDEN WORLD: the v10 report (26 October 2026)

What was asked: act as the channel's head of production, build the dataset, diagnose, decide what the channel is, fix
the voice, rebuild the opening, package every release, raise the bar at zero cost, make the safety rules checks, build
the learning loop, make three connected Reels, and prove it. What follows is what was done, with the numbers, and what
was not.

## 1. The channel brief (docs/CHANNEL_BRIEF_2026-10-26.md)

**The world smuggling trade, told one person at a time**, for men 18–34 in the US, Australia, the UK, Germany, Canada
and India who meet us only in the Reels tab (followers 0.0–0.2 % of viewers). Format "ONE SMUGGLER": 28–35 s,
half-revealed object on frame 0, deed-first hook, the catch by 9 s, the person by 12 s, consequence, callback, a
two-sided question and a named "NEXT FILE" sign-off. One case per Reel; runs of three connect through the sign-off.
Cadence: one a day at 4 pm Sydney, built in runs of three, two minutes of the owner's time per post.

## 2. The voice decision and its cost (docs/VOICE_TEST_2026-10-26.md)

Seven narrators rendered on the same 56-second passage, blinded on the review page. Measured, not listened: the
current `am_adam` sits at the flat end (7.1 semitones of pitch spread); `am_fenrir` directed has 12.0, the widest of
the free voices. **Decision: Kokoro `am_fenrir`, directed delivery, zero cost**, used on 101–103; `bm_george` is the
British alternative. Directed delivery is now an engine feature (`narrator_profile.directed`): per-line tempo
variation, numbers slowed, a breath before reveals, room tone between sentences. Chatterbox could not be installed
(PyTorch blocked by the network policy). ElevenLabs is costed and off: about $0.12 per Reel on the $5/month Starter
plan, $5–22 a month; it stays the only route to "a stranger can't tell in five seconds", which no free voice can
promise.

## 4. The measured baseline (analytics/reels.csv, 46 readings of 32 Reels)

Median views **1,805**; skip 28.6 %; 70 / 45 / 20 % still watching at 4 s / 10 s / end; average watch 41–57 % of
length; like rate 0.8 %; **shares 0 on 24 of 29, saves 0 on 18 of 30, follows 0**. Best Reel 4,054 views (73).
The wall is the share, not the scroll-stop.

## 5. The targets for the next ten Reels

Median views **3,600** (double), average watch **above 50 % of length**, skip ≤ 23 %, ≥ 65 % at 10 s, and the first
non-zero shares since 92. Each Reel gets a post-mortem at 48 h naming the one thing to change; a miss is named.

## 7. The toughest reading of my own work: what still wouldn't make a stranger follow

1. **The follow ask is five lines of text in a 2.9-second end scene.** Nobody reads five lines in three seconds; the
   "NEXT FILE" line is the fourth. The spoken callback never mentions the next file. If follows stay at zero at 48 h,
   the end card drops to three lines (question / send / NEXT FILE) and the next file gets spoken, at the cost of a
   second of loop. I left it because 25 % of 97's likes land on the end card as it is and the data should decide.
2. **The voice is still a voice you could clock.** The measured pick is the best free one; the owner's ears haven't
   judged it yet (the blind page exists for that). A paid voice remains the only certain fix.
3. **102's hook is passive.** "Was stuffed" is weaker than a doer-first hook; it is the honest one. If 102 skips worse
   than 101 and 103, that is the price of fairness and worth paying.
4. **The person turn has nine seconds.** Enough for who and one unexpected detail, not for why; a 32–34 s cut would
   give the person three more seconds, which v10 now allows and the next run should try on one Reel.
5. **Shares still have no mechanism beyond the story and the send line.** If the run clears the view target without
   sends, the next engine step is a spoken send line or an interactive beat ("would you have caught it?").

## 8. What was deliberately left out

Chatterbox (not installable here), a paid voice (declined for now), the owner's own voice (declined), multi-part
cases (sequels underperform; profile traffic is 0.1 %), maps and timelines as standard scenes (the one-person format
rarely needs them), photographic evidence (policy), a rename to "How Good" (nothing in the data argues for it), and
any posting (the owner posts after sign-off).

## 9. The first month after

Week 1: post 101–103 one a day at 4 pm Sydney; screenshot each at 48 h and 7 d (five screens); run the compile and the
three post-mortems; the owner answers the blind test. Week 2: act on the three post-mortems (end card, voice, length
are the likely verdicts), build the next run of three in the same format with one 33-s cut. Weeks 3–4: ten Reels in,
read the scoreboard against the targets; if the median has doubled on skip alone, go after sends with the spoken
send line; if shares are still zero at 3k+ views, enable the paid voice for one run as the controlled test.
