# Victims, accused people and platform safety: the policy (26 October 2026)

This is the written policy behind the checks in `pipeline/safety_qa.py`, which run before every export. A reel that
fails a check is not published to `output/`.

## People

1. **Victims are never the joke, the hook or the cover.** No victim's name is spoken unless they are a public figure in
   the story's own reporting, and never with a nickname, a pun or a laugh line. No dead or injured person is drawn.
   Animal suffering is not shown and not played for laughs.
2. **Accused people are "arrested", "charged" or "police say" until a court says otherwise.** They are never named,
   never drawn as a likeness (silhouettes tagged NOT A LIKENESS), and the words guilty, smuggler, thief, robber, drug
   dealer are not attached to them as fact. The gate looks for a proper name next to an accusing verb without
   "charged / accused / alleged / police say / pleaded / convicted / sentenced" in the same line.
3. **Convicted people may be named** when they pleaded guilty or were convicted AND are widely named in reputable
   press, and the reel says what the court decided. Children (under 18 at the time) are never named.
4. **Families** are not contacted, quoted or drawn. A reported quote appears only on a "wording as reported" card with
   its source, and only when two independent sources carry it.
5. **No speculation as fact.** "Reportedly", "per the navy", "police say" stay in the narration and the labels; the
   claims ledger in `episodes/<id>/research.md` names a source for every scene's claim keys, and the shot manifest
   check `every_shot_sourced_and_tagged` fails a scene with none.

## Pictures and sound

6. **Illustrated, not photographic.** No real photographs of people, bodies, crime scenes or evidence; no weapons
   pointed at anyone; no violence shown; no blood. The banned-word list in `safety_qa.py` catches gore words in the
   narration and labels.
7. **Music we own.** The score is generated by `pipeline/score.py`; no licensed track is ever muxed in. The gate fails
   if the mix references any audio file outside `out/<id>/` and the engine's own synthesis.
8. **Brands as plain text**, no logos or liveries copied.

## Instagram

9. **AI label on.** Narration and pictures are synthetic; the owner switches on Instagram's "AI info" label on every
   post (`post.md` carries the reminder; the gate fails a `post.md` without it).
10. **No sensitive-topic bait.** No drug-use instructions, no "how to" for smuggling methods beyond what was reported
    as found, no glamorising of cartels; the joke is always on the scheme and the catch.
11. **Caption hygiene.** ≤ 5 hashtags, no banned or spam hashtags, no engagement-bait phrasing of the kind
    Instagram demotes ("like if", "follow for follow"); the pinned comment's "vote 1/2/3" is a question, not a bribe.

## What we deliberately leave out

Child victims, sexual offences, terrorism as entertainment, open murder cases with living suspects, and any case where
the only hook is someone's suffering. Smuggling, fraud and heists carry the channel because the loser is a scheme.
