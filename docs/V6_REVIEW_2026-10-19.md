## C. Factual verification

Every narrated claim sits in `episodes/<id>/research.md` with two sources (URLs, dates) unless marked.

**83.** The brief given to the builder was wrong on two counts and was corrected before the render: the ring ran from
the Netherlands into **Britain** (not Spain), and the seizure was about **270 kg** (193 kg cocaine, 74 kg heroin), not a
tonne (NCA, AP via Fox, DutchNews, Guardian Nigeria, EMS1). "45 trips" is the NCA figure (AP says 44, one report 39):
labelled "at least 45 · NCA". Sentences are given individually (28/24/18 years in 2015; six more in 2016) because the
totals disagree between outlets. The "patient on crutches" is in three outlets; the "dummy on the stretcher" the brief
suggested was unsourced and dropped. Coffin van (A29, 13 Dec 2024): the four-year sentence comes from the Rotterdam
court's own release and the prosecution service. Unresolved: the Daily Mail gives 93 kg of cocaine against the NCA's 193;
the reel follows the NCA.

**84.** Core agreed by AZFamily, FOX 10, 12News and the Scottsdale PD release: a 41-year-old man, accused of using
acquaintances' out-of-town posts to pick empty homes and of cutting power to cameras, tracked to his office after a
break-in; "at least a dozen homes, police say". Not narrated because sources disagree: the loss ($72k–$400k+), the
burglary count (12–20+), the charge count, the city where he was tracked. He is never named, "police say" on every
claim, AS OF OCT 2026 label; the hook and headline were rewritten from "He robbed…" to "Police say he robbed…" before
render. Martinsburg 2009: no verdict found in any source, so "charged", not convicted. Washington DC 2010: guilty plea
Feb 2011, 44 months (NBC, FindLaw). Unresolved: "Instagram" specifically is FOX 10's court-record wording; AZFamily says
"social media". Re-check before posting.

**85.** Chillicothe, 12 Oct 2026: two local outlets agree on the core (asleep upstairs, lock missing, relative called
police, "just sleeping", burglary charge). They disagree on the call time and on whether the house was vacant, so the
reel says neither; nobody is named, including the complainant. Michigan basement-stairs case (WWJ/CBS Detroit, WXYZ): no
year in either extract, so none is shown. Oklahoma County, 1 Oct 2026 (KOCO, KFOR, Fox, ABC7, Police1): custody status
conflicts, so the reel says only that the licence named him. Hemel Hempstead (verified) was not used. Unresolved: the
Michigan case's date.

## D. Three highest-impact remaining weaknesses

1. **Frame 0 shows the object, not the crime, on two of three reels** (84 most; 83 needs the word "cocaine"). The data
   from 72–79 says frame-0 readability is the skip-rate lever; v6 fixed motion and size but not "is the act visible".
2. **Live-case hooks must open with attribution** ("Police say…"), which blunts the first second. Non-negotiable for an
   accused person, so the fix is case selection: lead with convicted or self-evident cases (bodycam, pleas) and keep the
   live accusation as item 2 or 3.
3. **Shares are unproven.** The persona send line and the "explainable revelation" structure are hypotheses; the seven
   v5 reels had 0 shares. Nothing can be claimed until the 72-h readings exist.

## F. Files and rules modified

- `pipeline/shot_manifest.py`: new `characters_act(sh, loop_end)`; `build()` passes `loop_end` for the last scene of an
  engagement-v6 episode. Rule change: a figure holding its pose counts as acting only in the v6 loop end scene and only
  under a keyed camera. Everything else unchanged.
- `series/true-crime/tools/shot_precheck.py` (new): runs the slideshow gate on the plan-only build, prints what to change.
- `series/true-crime/tools/disk_guard.py` (new): reclaims `stems/`, `precheck/`, `video_silent.mp4` of finished episodes
  (never `--keep` ones, never plan/timeline/mix/MP4), exits 1 under `--min-gb`.
- `tests/test_v6_production.py` (new): 4 tests covering both rules and the guard's dry run, keep list and "finished"
  definition.
- Render queue (`q6.sh`, scratchpad): disk guard → make_episodes → plan-only build → hook_check → shot_precheck →
  motion_precheck → produce.
- Builder brief: shot-size classes and the self-check command added.

## H. Experiment for the next ten reels: one variable per pair

Post each pair in the same slot on consecutive days, same series tone, same length band; read at 6 h and 72 h; compare
skip, kept @4 s / @10 s, end %, shares/1k, follows/1k. A pair agrees or it does not; two agreeing pairs change the rule.

| Pair | Variable | A | B |

| 88/89 | Length | 28 s cut | 31 s cut |

| 92/93 | Hook grammar | noun-first ("The Instagram burglar: police say…") | attribution-first ("Police say he…") |

## I. Recommendation

**Keep v6 as it is creatively, with the three production fixes merged, and investigate one specific defect:** the
"Oklahoma" garble (a pronunciation entry and a recogniser pass over every proper noun, not only the hook line). Do not
tighten the loop threshold (2.0): all three reels landed at 0.36–0.82 with motivated returns (doors, blink, torch) and
no ending was weakened to get there; its only real test is average watch time above reel length at 72 h. Let the 6-h
and 72-h readings of 83–85 against `retention/TRACKING_V6.md` decide the first creative change, starting with pair 86/87.
