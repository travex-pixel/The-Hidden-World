# THE HIDDEN WORLD: channel brief (26 October 2026)

One direction, chosen from `analytics/reels.csv` (32 Reels, 46 readings) and defended with it. Not a menu.

## Who we are for

Men aged 18–34 (93 % of viewers on 97 and 98; 95 % men), in the United States first (20–32 %), then Australia, the
UK, Germany, Canada and India, who reach us **only** through the Reels tab (91–96 % of views; followers 0.0–0.2 %).
Every Reel is a cold start shown to strangers; nothing we post can rely on anyone having seen the last one.

## The corner we own

**The world smuggling trade, told one person at a time.** The mules, the builders of cannons and submarines, the
officers who open the suitcase: real seizures and convictions, illustrated, 30 seconds, with the absurd object on
frame 0 and a human being behind it by second 10.

Why this corner and not another:
- The smuggling lane is the only one this audience reliably stops for: 8 of 9 smuggling/border Reels were pushed past
  Instagram's hour-1 seed test; generic burglars and getaways 2 of 7; wine heists under-indexed twice. The top three by
  views (73 fake Border Patrol truck 4,054; 92 drug cannon 2,980; 63 Jeep on the fence 2,920) are all in it.
- The only two Reels anyone ever **shared** are in the same lane and are stories about a person: 45 (an 87-year-old
  war hero caught hauling cartel cocaine: 13 shares, 6 saves, like rate 1.7 %) and 37 (the cocaine plane crashed in the
  Papua New Guinea jungle: 11 shares, the account's only follow from a Reel). The 35 object-list Reels since have
  5 shares between them. People send people.
- The object opening is solved: making-verb hooks ("Smugglers turned cocaine into a jacket") and a half-revealed
  object on frame 0 give 17–23 % skip and 60–65 % still watching at 10 s (97, 98). We keep that engine for the first
  nine seconds and spend the next twenty on the person.

## The signature Reel: ONE SMUGGLER (format v10)

| seconds | beat | what the data says |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Cold open, half-reveal.** Hands opening the object, inside still hidden; the spoken hook is the deed, ≤ 8 words, making verb, no number ("A 91-year-old flew cocaine into Sydney as soap.") | 17–23 % skip on every Reel built this way |
| 3–9 | **The gotcha.** How it was found; the object opens fully on this line with the one impact | the payoff by 9 s stopped the 4–10 s leak that cost a third of viewers on v5 |
| 9–20 | **The person.** Who did it, the detail nobody expects (age, job, the lie they told), one evidence close-up (a boarding pass, a court line, a customs tag) generated from the script | 45 and 37: the only share-earners, both one-person stories; mid-video like spikes land on a human being caught, never on a tonnage |
| 20–27 | **Consequence and irony.** The sentence, the quote as reported, the thing they said at the airport | the like spike on 45 at the sentence; 74's cliff shows a soft, inconclusive last line empties the end |
| 27–30 | **Callback + end card.** Back on the object; a two-sided question ("DUPED OR GUILTY? 👇"), "SEND TO THE MATE WHO'D…" (a person the viewer has), and the sign-off that earns the follow: **"NEXT FILE: <three words>"**, a named tease of the next Reel | 25 % of 97's likes land in its last second; followers are 0 %, so the only reason to follow is a specific next thing |

- **Length 28–35 s** (the 28-second cuts hold 57 % of their length; 37–38 s cuts ended at 25–28 %). Under 40 always.
- **On-screen text:** the same two-line serif headline on frame 0 (object first, never "POLICE SAY"), burned-in
  captions in our amber (#e8a33a) on near-black, large, never over the object; a case chip after 3 s; one counter.
- **How a case is split across parts: it isn't.** Sequels scored below their originals (76 < 73, 77 < 74), "PART N" on
  frame 1 costs a scroll, and nobody arrives from the profile. A case gets one Reel. Reels connect through the
  "NEXT FILE" sign-off and the pinned comment, so a run of three sends people along the chain without needing it.
- **Voice:** the blind test (docs/VOICE_TEST_2026-10-26.md) decides; the script is written for the ear either way:
  short sentences, contractions, a breath before every reveal, names and numbers slowed, rhythm varied by beat.
- **Captions carry the story with the sound off** (most first views are muted); the voice is the reward for sound on.

## Cadence the owner can sustain (zero hours, zero budget)

The engine builds in runs of three (research, script, art, voice, QA, covers, caption) and the owner posts one a day at
**4 pm Sydney** (the slot every pushed Reel since 86 went out in), spending about two minutes per post: caption, seed
comment, pinned comment, Instagram's AI label. Six Reels a week, no misses, and a 48-hour and 7-day screenshot set per
Reel (analytics/README.md) so the learning loop runs. Nothing else is asked of the owner.

## What we deliberately do not do

- No "Police say" openings, no questions as hooks, no year first (2 of 8 pushed).
- No tonnage-first hooks (28–34 % skip every time).
- No victims as the joke, no graphic imagery, no accused person named; all three stay engine checks, not guidelines.
- No multi-part cases, no "PART N" on frame 1, no reliance on followers.
- No stock footage, no music we do not own (the score is generated in `pipeline/score.py`).
