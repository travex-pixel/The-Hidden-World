## Fun fact (one card)
- **The Red Mansion was opened as an anti-corruption museum (Premier Zhu Rongji's order, SCMP) and shut in less than
  three weeks, officially for safety reasons.** SCMP ("Inside the Red Chamber"); Frommer's ("reopened briefly as an
  anticorruption museum but was quickly shuttered"); Wikipedia.

## Lesson (one card)
- "A 'gift' that buys a favor is a bribe. 64 officials took his, a court found." Practical, tied to the verified court
  finding (Reuters copy; AP).
