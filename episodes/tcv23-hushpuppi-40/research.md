## Established facts
- **Identity.** Ramon Abbas (also Olalekan / "Ray"), known online as Hushpuppi / "Ray Hushpuppi". [H_who: DOJ, BBC, NBC]
- **The flex.** He posted a lavish lifestyle on Instagram. Court papers (as reported) describe his accounts as frequently
  showing him in designer clothes and expensive watches, posing in or with luxury cars and **charter jets**; CNN cites
  Rolls-Royces. Narration says "posed with jets and luxury cars" — not "his private jets" (ownership not established). [H_flex]
- **Followers.** BBC: 2.5 million Instagram followers; CNN: more than two million. Narration: "over two million". [H_followers]
- **How the account helped (FBI affidavit, as reported in 2020).** Agents obtained Instagram's records for his verified
  account: an email address and a verified phone number. The same email was on the Snapchat account "hushpuppi5";
  a phone belonging to an accomplice had a UAE number saved as "Hush" tied to that Snapchat name, and his Instagram
  mentioned the same Snapchat name. Apple records for a linked iCloud address listed a Dubai address. Agents matched his
  posted photos to photos from his passports and other ID. [H_records, H_snap, H_passport: Quartz, The Register, IBTimes, CNN]
  Wording: "His own account helped the FBI identify him" — not "caught him" (Dubai Police made the arrest).
- **Arrest.** Dubai Police arrested him in June 2020 (reports give 10 June; police announced it ~25-26 June) in
  simultaneous raids, including his Dubai apartment; 12 people in total. Police said they seized cash and 13 luxury cars
  (about Dh25m). The cash figure differs between outlets (The National/CNN: ~Dh150m / ~$41m cash; Dubai Eye describes items
  worth more than Dh150m incl. computers and phones), so **no cash amount is narrated or shown**. [H_raid]
- **"Fox Hunt 2"** was the Dubai Police name for the operation (fun-fact card). [H_foxhunt: Dubai Eye, Khaleej Times, The National]
- **Hand-over.** Dubai Police handed him to the FBI; FBI agents brought him to the US; first court appearance in Chicago,
  3 July 2020, then Los Angeles. The DOJ told the BBC he was expelled, not extradited (the UAE has no extradition treaty
  with the US). Card says "Flown to the US · July 2020". [H_handover: Gulf News, Graphic/BBC, Channels TV]
- **Plea.** April 2021 (unsealed July 2021): pleaded guilty to one count of conspiracy to engage in money laundering —
  laundering money from business email compromise and other online fraud schemes. [H_plea: DOJ, BBC, NBC]
- **Sentence.** November 2022 (Mon 7 Nov), US District Judge Otis D. Wright II: 135 months in federal prison (over 11 years)
  and $1,732,841 restitution to two victims. Narration: "more than eleven years". [H_sentence: DOJ, NBC, FOX 5 NY]
- **Status as of 12 Oct 2026.** The Ninth Circuit dismissed his petition for review (judgment 19 Jan 2024; reported
  29 Jan 2024). Fact-checks in Feb 2026 (Africa Check, TheCable) found viral claims of his release/deportation false; he
  remains in US federal custody (BOP inmate locator, as reported: FCI Fort Dix; projected release 2029, TheCable: April 2029).
  No credible report of release, new charges or a successful appeal found. Not narrated. [H_status]
