## Corrections to the brief
- **Weight of the cast:** the brief said the cast was "about 5 kg". Police said the cast itself weighed **about a kilogram**; **about 5 kg** (RNZ: 4.85 kg) was the TOTAL with the beer cans and the stool legs. The reel says "5 kilos, cocaine inside six beer cans" and the callout says "the cast itself, about 1 kg".
- **"Seat covers":** no extract mentions seat covers. The other hiding places reported are **six emptied, repacked and resealed beer cans** and **the legs of two small folding stools**. Only the beer cans are spoken; the stools are in the ledger.
- **How officers spotted it:** the brief suggested the X-ray, the weight or the profile. The reports say police found the drug by **spraying the cast with a chemical reagent that turns bright blue on contact with cocaine**; the X-rays are reported for the fracture, not the detection. The gotcha line uses the spray.
- **The leg:** confirmed. X-rays showed a real fracture (Emol/EFE: an open fracture of the left tibia and fibula); investigators examined the possibility that the injury was self-inflicted to get through security. That is stated on screen as a possibility they examined, not a finding.
- **The man:** 66 years old, Chilean, arriving from Santiago; the brief did not give his age. Never named anywhere in the reel.
- **Frozen sharks' weight:** the AP (citing prosecutors) gives 893 kg (1,965 lb) in 870 packages; Reuters/Al Jazeera "more than a ton"; a Foreign Policy blog 867 kg; La Tercera 394 kg (an outlier not used). Spoken as "nearly a ton" (AP's own headline: "Almost one ton"), with 893 kg and the Reuters figure both on the label.
