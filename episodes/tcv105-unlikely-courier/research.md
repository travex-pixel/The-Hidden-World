Other angles checked and dropped: great-grandmothers (no 2015-2026 conviction with an object found); teachers and

**Chosen: Gildo Ricci, 69, of Rockville, Maryland, and the cakes.** He landed at Washington Dulles from Bogota on
25 April 2024 with a suitcase of desserts and food. One cake was unusually heavy; a customs officer probed it with a
knife and **the knife would not go in**. Under the top layer was a foil-wrapped brick. In all, 2.07 kg of cocaine was in
his cakes and soup-mix packets. A backpack in his suitcase held $7,750 that turned out to be counterfeit. A federal jury
in Alexandria convicted him in January 2025 of possession with intent to distribute cocaine.

Why it qualifies: one person; a concluded outcome (jury conviction, announced by the US Attorney's Office); a striking
object that is not on the excluded list (a cake); a half-reveal hands can do on frame 0 (a knife that will not go into
a cake, the inside hidden); a human detail nobody expects (69, a suitcase of cakes, chocolates and soup mix, and fake
cash in a backpack); nothing from Case Files 37-104 (no cake, Dulles or Ricci case in any slate).

The "unlikely courier" here is honest but modest: a 69-year-old flying home with sweets is the person nobody would
search. The record says nothing about grandchildren, his job or his story to officers, so the reel claims none of it.

**Tease for Case File 104's sign-off (105 is not a bible case): "NEXT FILE: THE COCAINE CAKE".**

## The story in order

1. **Who he was.** Gildo Ricci, 69, a Peruvian citizen with US permanent residence, living in Rockville, Maryland. The
   sources give nothing else (job, family, why he travelled).
2. **The trip.** Bogota to Washington Dulles, landing about 6:45 am on 25 April 2024, his suitcases full of chocolates,
   cakes and soup mixes.
3. **The check.** Customs sent him to secondary inspection. One cake was unusually heavy. The officer pushed a knife into
   it and the knife would not go in. Under the top layer: a foil-wrapped brick.
4. **The rest.** The other cakes and the soup-mix packets held cocaine too: 2.07 kg in all. A backpack in his suitcase
   held $7,750 in counterfeit bills.
5. **The outcome.** He went to trial. A federal jury in Alexandria convicted him in January 2025 of possession with
   intent to distribute. (Sentence and appeal: single source, not narrated.)

## The object (frame 0 and the gotcha)

A round layer cake on a steel inspection table, close. A blue-gloved hand presses a knife down into the top and the
blade will not sink (it flexes, the cake does not give); the other hand steadies the cake; a short cut line in the top
shows only a dark seam. On the gotcha the top layer is lifted off on a silver foil-wrapped brick (the single impact).
**Nothing verified describes the cake's colour, size, decoration or the brick's size.** Draw a generic frosted cake and

**Evidence close-up (person beat):** our own typed customs inventory tag (no real form imitated): "BACKPACK · $7,750"
with "COUNTERFEIT" stamped across it (R6).
