## Allegations (attributed, not narrated)
- Police allege the cocaine was landed by boat near Midge Point, North Queensland, and moved south for a Sydney organised crime group (Operation Minjiang); the alleged mother vessel MV Wealth was detained in the Solomon Islands; six other people charged. (ABF release; AP; InDaily.)

## Part 2 tease (end card, verified)
- "PART 2: 2.3 TONNES ON A FISHING BOAT": Australia's previous record, 2.34 tonnes of cocaine seized from a fishing boat near K'gari (Fraser Island), Queensland, in 2024. (AP; NPR.)
