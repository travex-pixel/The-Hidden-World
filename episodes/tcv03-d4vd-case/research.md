# Research dossier: the D4vd case (People v. David Anthony Burke)

**Legal status verified on 2026-10-11.** This case is ongoing. David Anthony Burke ("D4vd") has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent. Every accusation here is the prosecution's allegation, not an established fact.

## Method and limits
- Sources: about 45 web searches by a research pass (standard and extended mode), plus independent re-checks by the producer of every on-screen claim. The re-checks are listed in "Producer verification" below.
- Direct page fetching (WebFetch, curl) is blocked in this environment, and the search tool refused latimes.com. Every claim therefore rests on search-result extracts, cross-checked across independent outlets.
- Ranking of sources: official LA County District Attorney (LADA) releases, then AP/Reuters (via syndicators), then LA broadcast outlets (ABC7, NBC LA, KTLA, FOX 11, CBS LA), then national and trade outlets (CNN, ABC News, Deadline, Variety, Rolling Stone).
- Tabloid-only claims are excluded (see "Do not use").

## Prosecution allegations (attributed, unproven)
- **20 Apr 2026, DA Nathan Hochman.** One count each of:
  - murder, with special-circumstance allegations of lying in wait, financial gain and killing a witness;
  - continuous sexual abuse of a child under 14;
  - unlawful mutilation of human remains.

  Sources: LADA release, ABC News, Deadline.
- **Alleged timeline.** Prosecutors allege Celeste went to Burke's Hollywood Hills home on 23 April 2025, at his invitation, and was not seen alive after that date. She was 14 on that date and turned 15 on 7 Sept 2025. Sources: LADA, ABC13/ABC News, NewsNation (Hochman: "The allegation, as alleged in the complaint, is that Burke invited Celeste to his house the night of April 23, 2025…").
- **Death penalty.** Burke is eligible because of the special circumstances. Hochman: "At this point, the death penalty determination has not been made." (ABC45, July 2026; AP, Aug 2026).

## Defence positions (attributed)
- **April 2026, former counsel Blair Berk, Marilyn Bednarski and Regina Peter.** They said the evidence would show Burke "did not murder Celeste Rivas Hernandez" and vowed to "vigorously defend David's innocence". Sources: Reuters via Interaksyon; Rolling Stone, Q107.
- **27 Jul 2026, Bednarski's preliminary-hearing closing.** "There's no evidence that Mr. Burke harbored a deliberate intent to kill" (Spectrum News, ABC7). Not used on screen.
- **31 Aug 2026.** The private lawyers withdrew, and the Public Defender's Office (Walid Kandeel, Lisa Anne Roth) was appointed. Sources: AP via PBS, ABC7, NBC LA.

- **Next date.** ABC7/ABC News and NBC LA give 19 Oct; ABC7 NY lists 19 Oct. - **Recent filings.** No October 2026 criminal-case development was found.

## Unresolved or disputed (not presented as fact)
- No trial date has been set. The death-penalty decision is pending.
- Whether 19 Oct proceeds, is continued, or sets a trial date.
- Toxicology and pregnancy reporting conflicts between outlets. Both are omitted.
- Why the private lawyers withdrew, and the family attorney's dispute over indigency. Both are omitted.
- The civil wrongful-death suit (Sept 2026) is separate from the criminal case and contains unproven allegations. It is omitted.

## Do not use
- Graphic forensic details from the preliminary hearing.
- Talon Marks, TMZ-only and other tabloid claims.
- Social-media speculation and aggregator sites.
- Any wording that calls Burke the killer or presents the allegations as fact.
- Any claim that the death penalty "will be sought", or that a trial date was set.

## Production and ethics decisions
- No real person is depicted. Everyone on screen is an anonymous silhouette: no likeness of Burke, Celeste, her family, the judge, the DA or the lawyers.
- The victim and the remains are never shown. The tow yard, the house and the courthouse are generic illustrations, each tagged ILLUSTRATION.
- No imitation of official documents, police, evidence or court material. Cards are the series' own typed graphics.
- Name pronunciation: "D4vd" is pronounced "David". The narration says "David" while captions show the written stage name.
- Respect for the family: the video ends on the presumption of innocence and on the family still waiting. There is no sensational sign-off.
