## Corrections to the brief
- **Outcome of the bridge case:** the brief said both men "later pleaded guilty/were sentenced". Three searches (including an extended one and one aimed at the New Castle News) found NO plea, verdict or sentence in any report; every result stops at the October 2011 charges and the $25,000 bond. The brothers are therefore CHARGED only: never named on screen, in narration, in the caption or in the seed comment; drawn ONLY as silhouettes tagged NOT A LIKENESS; "police say"; AS OF OCT 2026. The headline "THEY STOLE / AN ENTIRE BRIDGE" names nobody (that a bridge was cut up, sold as scrap and bought by a recycling company is established); the spoken hook carries the attribution ("Police say two brothers…"). Flagged as an open risk in the report.
- **Dimensions and weight:** most outlets say 50 by 20 feet; one said 40 by 15. The reel says "fifty feet" (spoken) and "about 50 ft" (label). The complaint's figure is 31,000 lb of steel grate and I-beams (about 15.5 tons); one outlet described it as a 40-ton bridge; the reel uses the complaint's 31,000 lb only.
- **Scrap value:** $5,179 per the complaint ("about $5,000" in several extracts). The callback says "scrapped for five" (five grand).
- **Bridge value:** state police put the bridge's value at about $100,000; the owner said replacing it would cost approximately $100,000. The callback says "worth a hundred grand".
- **Beach volume:** the "500 truckloads" figure is the police estimate reported in 2008; the Mines and Geology Division later said no more than 140. The label gives both, attributed; the narration says only "a beach".
- **Beach outcome:** a Gleaner extract (11 Dec 2008) reports two arrests after a raid on a Negril company, and one blog says five people were charged with simple larceny and the charges were dropped in February 2011 after the complaining witness was threatened. Neither is confirmed by a second independent extract, so the reel claims NO arrest and NO outcome: it says what the October 2008 reporting says ("no sand, no suspects") and labels "no conviction found in reports, as of Oct 2026".
- **Glacier quantity and place:** outlets give 5, 5.5 and nearly 6 tons / 11,453 lb; the reel says "five tons" (the most common figure). The arrest is placed near Cochrane (Capitán Prat province, Aysén) by one outlet and in "Patagonia" by the rest; the label says PATAGONIA, CHILE.
- **"Tons" vs "tonnes":** sources mix them; the narration says "tons" for the recogniser, the label says "tonnes".
