# Story notes — Episode 004: The Compliment With a Deadline

**Format:** fictional psychological thriller, about 40 seconds. No real people, companies or events.

## Hook options (three drafted, one chosen)
1. **"You're the smartest person in this building."** Extreme close-up, warm light, eyes flick down then back. *Chosen:* flattering and slightly wrong at once.
2. A phone lighting up "Sign it tonight" over a dinner table.
3. Her pen hovering over a signature line, then the cut back to "three weeks earlier".

## Psychological basis (no factual claim is made on screen)
- **Conditional approval:** warmth given when someone complies and withdrawn when they push back, which trains compliance without an explicit demand.
- **Flattery before a request:** praise raises the cost of refusing, because refusing now feels like disappointing someone who believes in you.
- **Not every compliment is manipulation.** The closing line separates the two: a real compliment asks for nothing; the warning sign is praise attached to a request and a deadline.

## Agency check
Mara enjoys the praise (a believable flaw), notices the pattern herself, and acts before the scene in which she is pressured. The reversal is her decision, not a rescue.
