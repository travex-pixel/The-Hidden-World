# Case File 100 — A Tunnel Under a Fake Store (BORDER FAILS · 7)

Research ledger, built 24–25 October 2026. Engine v9, lane: border. The milestone reel. Series rule for this one: things
smugglers BUILT to beat a land border, each found by a named agency, so each is a settled fact (the tunnel, the zip line,
the bundles exist and were found). Nobody accused is named; the four men charged in San Diego are not drawn at all; the
men arrested in Douglas and Lynden appear only as silhouettes.

## Method and limits

- Web search only (standard and extended modes), about 14 queries, 24–25 Oct 2026. Full pages could not be opened from the
  build environment (WebFetch returned a DNS error for ktla.com, fox11online.com and justice.gov), so every claim below
  rests on **search-result extracts**, cross-checked between at least two independent outlets. The FOX-owned stations
  (LiveNOW, KTVU, FOX 29 and others) syndicate one story and count as ONE source; FOX 5 San Diego (a Nexstar station) is
  separate from them. The DOJ release and the ICE/HSI release are government sources, counted separately from the press.
- Access date for every URL: 2026-10-25 (search-result extract).
- Where outlets disagree on a number (the tunnel's length, the arrest counts), the reel uses only what two of them agree
  on, or the safe rounding ("nearly 2,000 feet" for 1,933 ft).

## Statistic (counter)

1,933 FT — the tunnel's length as prosecutors gave it (U.S. Attorney via the FOX stations, KESQ, FOX 5 San Diego). The
narration rounds it as "nearly 2,000 feet" (FOX 5's headline uses "2,000-foot"; KTLA/LiveNOW "more than a third of a mile").

## Fun fact (SUCCESs)

The Lynden tunnel was the first ever found under the US–Canada border (CNN; The Globe and Mail: "the first covert tunnel
ever discovered between Canada and the U.S."; HistoryLink).

## Corrections to the brief and claims NOT used

- **The brief's candidate (El Paso storm drain, January 2025) was verified but not picked:** CBS News and Yahoo/WSAV agree it
  was found on 10 January 2025, ran from Ciudad Juárez into a public storm drain in El Paso, about 6 ft by 4 ft, with
  lighting, ventilation and wooden shoring, its hole covered by a metal plate. But the sources disagree on how it was found
  (a tip vs a routine CBP inspection) and nobody was caught, so it has no gotcha. The fake-store tunnel (June 2026) is newer,
  better sourced, has the better half-reveal (a floor hatch in a shop) and a gotcha two sources agree on.
- **Cartel attribution (CJNG):** only in secondary outlets (Borderland Beat, SOFX, 10News "authorities believe"); not in the
  DOJ release extract. NOT used.
- **The store's real name** ("Buy 4 Less") is in the ledger only; the illustration shows a plain SALE banner, no brand.
- **The four defendants' names** are in the DOJ release; NOT used (charged, not convicted).
- **"No customers":** the reports say "few customers" / "not seeing many customers" / "minimal foot traffic", so the
  narration says "barely any customers", not "no customers". (A first draft, "almost nobody shopped there", was heard by the
  recogniser as "nobody shot there" and was reworded.)
- **The freezers:** the packages were loaded into three deep freezers on a truck away from the storeroom (a mechanic shop
  nearby per one extract); deputies stopped that truck and 173 packages were seized from it, the rest of the cocaine coming
  from a second truck and a van. The callback says "Deputies found cocaine packed in freezers" (true of that truck), not that
  all of it was in freezers; the inset card credits prosecutors. (A first draft, "Prosecutors say the cocaine rode in
  freezers.", made the end scene too long and had no outcome word; replaced.)
- **2026 Nogales tunnel (259 ft, drainage-based)** and the **Tijuana house tunnel (June 2026, ~265 m)**: single or
  less-authoritative sources, or Mexican-side only; not used.
- **Zip line value ($120,000+)** and the CBP release wording: one extract; not used. The man's court outcome: not found;
  he is shown as arrested only.
- **Lynden arrest count:** DOJ says three charged; AP/Deseret reported up to five arrests including two roadside arrests on
  16 July. The reel says three (the DOJ figure). The discovery tip's source (neighbours/RCMP) was not found; not used.
- Not used (already made): the KFC tunnel, the 2020 elevator and 2022 rail tunnels to Otay Mesa (Case File 76), the drug
  cannon, catapult, fence Jeep, ultralight, drone, fake Border Patrol truck.

## Ethics decisions

- The hook states the build (a fact: the tunnel exists and was found), agent-first with a making verb: "Smugglers built a
  tunnel under a fake store." No accused person is in the hook; "smugglers" refers to the scheme, not to a named person.
- The San Diego defendants are charged, not convicted: never named, never drawn; the freezer detail is attributed
  ("Prosecutors say"). The Douglas man and the Lynden men are never named or given a face (dark rim-lit silhouettes, NOT A
  LIKENESS).
- Agents are generic figures without insignia; no weapons; a torch only; no force shown.
- No logos, plates or real interfaces: the store, freezer, boxes, hut and house are illustrations; the hidden-camera frame is
  our own typed graphic.
- The joke is on the builds (a shop nobody shopped in, a zip line over the fence, a tunnel into a living room), never on a
  person.
