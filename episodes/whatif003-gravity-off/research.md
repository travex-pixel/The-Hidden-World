# Fact check: "What If Gravity Switched Off for 10 Seconds?" (What-If 003)

**Accessed:** 2026-10-14.

## Method and limitations (read first)
* **Snippets only:** every source was seen only as a search-result snippet; pages could not be opened from the sandbox.
  No URL was invented.
* **The scenario is impossible.** Gravity cannot switch off. The film is a cartoon thought experiment and says so on
  screen (DRAMATISED), in the description and here.
* **Our own arithmetic is labelled ESTIMATE on screen** and shown below with its working. Anything beyond it (the air
  and sea coming back down, the planet's interior) is labelled HYPOTHETICAL.

## The physics, with working
* **Why nothing happens at first (G3).** With gravity gone, a standing person feels no force except the ground. The
  ground, however, is moving in a circle with the spin and needs a centripetal acceleration v²/r to do so. At Sydney
  (latitude 34° S), v ≈ 1,670 × cos 34° ≈ 1,385 km/h ≈ 385 m/s; v²/r = 385² / 6.371 × 10⁶ ≈ 0.023 m/s². Over 10 s that
  is ½ × 0.023 × 100 ≈ 1.2 m. At the equator it is 1.7 m. So you would drift up about a metre, slowly.
* **Why the air and sea take off (G4).** The atmosphere and the ocean sit in hydrostatic balance: dP/dz = −ρg, i.e.
  the upward pressure-gradient force on each parcel equals its weight. Remove gravity and that upward force is
  unbalanced, so each parcel accelerates upward at g ≈ 9.8 m/s². After 10 s: speed ≈ 98 m/s (350 km/h), rise ≈ 490 m;
  after 8 s: ≈ 310 m. This treats the fluid as a whole column with its surface pressure fixed, which is a cartoon
  simplification; it gives the right order of magnitude for the first seconds.
* **Why people are then carried up (G5).** A belly-to-earth skydiver's terminal speed is about 54–55 m/s (≈200 km/h):
  at that airspeed drag equals a person's weight. Once the updraft exceeds it (after about 5.6 s at 1 g), drag on a
  person exceeds their (now zero) weight by a wide margin: the air lifts them.
* **The Moon (G8).** Mean orbital speed 1.022 km/s (NASA). In 10 s it moves ≈ 10 km; without gravity it goes
  straight instead of curving, a deviation far smaller than that over an orbit ≈ 384,000 km in radius.
* **Gravity's weakness (G10).** Gravity is by far the weakest of the four fundamental forces; a small magnet lifts a
  paperclip against the pull of the whole Earth (a widely used illustration, attributed to Lisa Randall among others).

## Not used
* **Times Square crowds:** the Alliance's 2023 average of 283,090 pedestrians a day was checked (S11) but is not stated
  in the film.
* **"Every pigeon in Westminster"** and "a number 24 bus" are colour, not claims (the 24 is a real route; nothing is
  claimed about it).
* **Weather records** for the returning air are not claimed; the film says "beats any storm on record" only as a
  labelled cartoon line under a HYPOTHETICAL badge.
* **Earth's interior:** also held together by gravity and under enormous pressure; what it would do in ten seconds is
  not modelled and the film says so.
