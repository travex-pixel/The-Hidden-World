**Disagreements recorded:**
- **Escape count:** 18 (his own count, two sources) vs "almost twenty" (Grann) vs 16 (one low-quality page). Attributed to him.
- **His age at San Quentin:** some pages say 70; birth date 23 June 1920 and San Quentin News give 59. 59 used.
- **When he was caught after 1979:** pages give "four years" (1983, the Over-the-Hill Gang arrest) or "twenty-one years". The episode says only "Twenty years later, at 78, he was still robbing banks", which both support.
- **Final arrest year:** Tampa Bay Times (May 1999) and Grann: April 1999 at 78. One page (and part of Wikipedia's summary) says 2000 at 79. 1999 used.
- **The guard:** one account (AMC) says a tower guard asked if they needed help and they said they were fine; another (SF Chronicle, as summarised) has a different wording. Only one extract was traceable to a URL, so it is **not used**.

## Lesson (checklist card with SAVE ribbon, captions off)
Spoken cue: "Caught in a bank robbery? Save these three rules."
Rows: "STAY CALM. DO AS TOLD." / "NOTE THEIR FACE AND HEIGHT" / "TOUCH NOTHING THEY TOUCHED"; credit "Missouri State Highway Patrol · NCJRS (US DOJ)".
- Stay calm and cooperate fully with the robber's commands: Missouri State Highway Patrol brochure SHP-555D https://apps.mshp.dps.mo.gov/MSHPWeb/Publications/Brochures/documents/SHP-555D.pdf ; NCJRS training abstract (calm, polite, cooperative) https://ojp.gov/ncjrs/virtual-library/abstracts/bank-robbery
- Observe the robber's facial features and height: MSHP brochure; OHS Insider https://ohsinsider.com/safe-work-procedures-for-preventing-dealing-with-robberies/?print=pdf
- Do not touch objects or surfaces the robber touched (fingerprints, DNA): San Diego County Sheriff, Bank Robbery Procedures https://apps.sdsheriff.net/PublicDocs/SB978/Law%20Enforcement%20Services%20Bureau/Field%20Operations%20Manual/POLICY%2017_Bank%20Robbery%20Procedures_Redacted.pdf ; OHS Insider.
