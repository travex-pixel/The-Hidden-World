## Myths and claims NOT used
- "Built" (the brief's hook): the sources say "cloned", "made to look like", "decal stickers replicating", so the hook says
  "made a fake", not "built" (nothing says a vehicle was built from scratch).
- The "Border Patron" misspelt clone (one CBS item only): not found again, not used.
- The Rio Grande Valley agent's line that real units have "no fender, no ground effect": a general remark, not a detail
  of any one fake, so not used.
- 2015 Laredo fake Border Patrol Tahoe (12 passengers) and the 2024 Arizona fake van: verified but not needed (list of 3).
- Migrant counts for the K-9 case (24 vs 25): not narrated.
- Fake ambulances: real ambulances, not disguised vehicles; not used.

## Ethics decisions
- The joke is on the smugglers' disguises, never on the people being smuggled: they are never drawn (the people found with
  the truck stay out of frame), never counted as cargo, and the card says they were detained, as reported.
- The fence-cutter is an anonymous dark silhouette. The El Paso suspects and the three K-9 defendants (charged, no
  outcome found) are never named or drawn.
- Agents and officers are generic figures with no badge, patch, seal, flag or agency name; they hold only flashlights.
- The fakes carry only plain lettering ("BORDER PATROL", "K-9") and a plain green stripe, as reported; no seal or crest;
  the copied plate is drawn as blank dots, never a number.
- FedEx is plain text only; the vans are plain white with no logo, colours or artwork; nothing implies the company had any part.
