## Corrections to the brief
- **Stores and states:** the thefts police link him to were at several **Walmart** stores in **Wisconsin and Illinois** (two states). The brief said "multi-state" without naming them.
- **The arrest:** the plate-reader alert put officers nearby; Walmart staff on McCoy Road, Sun Prairie, then reported an active theft. He **fled on foot** and was caught **in a backyard** after a short chase. He was not stopped in a car.
- **Number of sets:** no outlet reports how many sets. The reel gives no count for this case. Officers recovered about **$1,500** of LEGO products.
- **Charges:** the outlets differ. WMTV/WFRV list retail theft, resisting or obstructing an officer, and bail jumping. One outlet lists attempted retail theft over $500. He also had a nationwide extraditable warrant on two burglary counts and two theft counts. The on-screen label says only "ACCUSED · RETAIL THEFT, RESISTING, BAIL JUMPING".
- **Camera wording:** WFRV's headline says "Flock cameras". The other outlets say "automatic license plate reader". Narration says "plate cameras". "Flock" appears only as a search term and in a sourced label.
- **Frame 1:** the car boot crammed with boxes is an illustrated sound-off picture of the case, not a reported scene. No outlet says boxes were found in his car. It carries the tag ILLUSTRATION.
