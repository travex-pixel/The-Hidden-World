# War room — Episode 008: *You Can See Through Your Microwave Door. The Microwaves Can't.*

1. **CTO:**
   - The glass isn't the shield; microwaves pass through glass.
   - The perforated metal sheet is the shield. Its 1–2 mm holes are about 80 times smaller than the 12.2 cm wave, so to the wave it's solid metal and the wave reflects.
   - Light, at about 0.5 µm, is about 3,000 times smaller than a hole and goes straight through.
2. **CEO hooks (picked #1):**
   1. "You think the glass door stops the microwaves. It doesn't."
   2. "You can see through your microwave door. The microwaves can't."
   3. "Your microwave door is full of holes. On purpose."
   4. "Why doesn't your microwave cook your face?"
   5. "That dotted screen is the only thing between you and 2.45 GHz."

   #1 has a clean false belief, and #2 is used as the title. #4 is clickbait-adjacent and implies danger, so it was rejected.
3. **Sceptic (22.7 s, middle of 39.2 s):** "Then why does my Wi-Fi drop when it's on?" Answered on screen: same band (2.45 vs 2.4 GHz), a tiny legal leak (US limit shown, with a note that the leak is drawn larger than life), and the Wi-Fi bars falling from 4 to 1. The shield works, and Wi-Fi is just that faint.
