## Method and limits

- 17 web searches on 2026-10-12, all in "standard" mode. That is two over the brief's budget of 15; the last two were the
  pronunciation check and the 2026 status check, which found the July 2026 petition (below). WebFetch and curl are blocked
  from the build environment, so every source is a **search-result extract** (title, URL and summary text). Full articles
  were not read. Dates come from the URL or the extract.
- There was **no trial**. Every clue comes from the probable-cause affidavit (released 5 Jan 2023), prosecutors' later
  filings or court rulings. The narration attributes them ("prosecutors said", "court filings said") and the cards say
  "PER THE AFFIDAVIT" or "COURT FILINGS".
- No quote, date, ruling or source was invented. The only words in quotation marks in this ledger are from the extracts.
  None is spoken or shown on screen.
- "No ruling found" means the searches returned nothing newer. It does not prove that nothing happened.

## Legal status (re-checked 2026-10-12)

- **2 Jul 2025:** pleaded guilty to four counts of first-degree murder and one count of felony burglary, weeks before an
  August 2025 trial. In exchange, prosecutors dropped the death penalty, and he waived his right to appeal. Sources:
  KATV (Sinclair/CBS2); Idaho Press/Boise Weekly; KVIA (CNN) 2025-06-30.
- **23 Jul 2025:** Judge Steven Hippler sentenced him to four consecutive life sentences without parole, plus 10 years
  for burglary. He declined to speak ("I respectfully decline") and gave no motive. Sources: Straight Arrow News; NBC
  News live blog; Coeur d'Alene Press; CBS News; Courthouse News; ABC17 (CNN) 2025-07-29.
- **27 Jul 2026 (NEW; not in the producer's notes):** he reportedly filed a post-conviction relief petition to withdraw
  his guilty plea, claiming "actual innocence" and saying the plea rested on "coercion, disinformation, false promises".
  This was reported alongside his first interview since sentencing (New York Times). Post-conviction relief is not an
  appeal, so the appeal waiver does not bar it. Sources: KCTV5 (Gray) 2026-07-27; Coeur d'Alene Press 2026-07-27
  ("per NYT report"); The Gazette. **No ruling was found** in the 2026-10-12 search. The convictions and sentence stand
  unless a court rules otherwise. Narration: "In 2026, he reportedly filed to withdraw his plea, claiming innocence."
  On screen: "PETITION REPORTED · NO RULING FOUND (12 OCT 2026)".
- **Civil suit (not narrated):** the four victims' estates are suing Washington State University for wrongful death and
  negligence, alleging that it ignored warnings about him. WSU denies responsibility. A federal jury trial in Seattle
  is set for 13 Sep 2027. Sources: KIVI https://www.kivitv.com/university-of-idaho-murders/trial-set-for-2027-in-university-of-idaho-victims-families-lawsuit-against-washington-state-university ;
  FOX 13 Seattle https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/families-idaho-students-bryan-kohberger-sue-wsu ; Court TV
  https://www.courttv.com/?p=153645 . This is about the university, not the clue chain, so it was left out to keep the
  episode short.

## Allegations: the clue chain (attributed: affidavit, prosecutors or court filings; never tested at trial)

1. **Knife sheath (K_sheath).** Investigators found a tan leather knife sheath at the scene. The affidavit says it was on
   a bed beside one victim and was stamped "Ka-Bar", "USMC" with the Marine Corps insignia. None of those details are
   used, the knife is never shown, and the sheath is described in words only. Sources: TODAY (NBC) 2023-01-05; NBC News
   2023-01-05; WFLA (Nexstar); Philadelphia Inquirer.
2. **DNA on the snap (K_snap).** The Idaho State Lab found "a single source of male DNA (suspect profile)" on the button
   snap. Narration: "On its snap was one man's DNA." Sources: TODAY; NBC News; WFLA.
3. **White car on video (K_car_video).** Neighbourhood video showed a white sedan near the house, making several passes,
   between about 3:29 and 4:20 a.m. on 13 Nov. The FBI identified it as a white Hyundai Elantra. Extracts differ on the
   earlier timings (2:50 or 2:53 a.m.) and on a camera address, so neither is used. Sources: Moscow-Pullman Daily News
   2023-01-05; KTVZ timeline; Fox News; 6abc (AP).
4. **Genetic genealogy (K_igg).** Prosecutors' June 2023 filings said the FBI used investigative genetic genealogy to build
   a family tree from the sheath DNA, then sent local police a tip to look at Kohberger. Police did not use the genealogy
   to obtain any warrant, the filings said. Narration: "Then, court filings said, genetic genealogy led the FBI to one
   family. And to Bryan Kohberger..." Sources: NBC News 2023-06; FOX 5 NY; NewsNation.
5. **His Elantra (K_elantra).** On 29 Nov 2022 a WSU police officer found a white 2015 Hyundai Elantra registered to him.
   Sources: 6abc (AP); CBS Philadelphia; Moscow-Pullman Daily News; WFLA.
6. **Phone records (K_phone).** The affidavit said his phone used cell coverage for the area of the house at least 12
   times before the killings. Extracts differ on when that started (June or August 2022), so no start date is given. The
   phone was near the house again hours after the killings, on the same morning; that is not used. Sources: 6abc (AP);
   CBS News; WFLA.
7. **The trash (K_trash).** On 27 Dec 2022, investigators in Pennsylvania took trash from the curb outside his parents'
   home in the Poconos. One extract says the trash reached the Idaho lab on 28 Dec. The episode gives no day in the
   narration; the card says "DEC 2022". Sources: NBC News 2023-06; TODAY; NBC News 2023-01; Philadelphia Inquirer.
8. **The paternity result (K_paternity), the payoff.** The lab found that DNA from the trash belonged to the biological
   father of the person who left the sheath DNA; "at least 99.9998% of the male population would be expected to be
   excluded" as that person's father. Narration: "Prosecutors said the trash DNA came from the father of whoever left
   the sheath." Card: "AT LEAST 99.9998% OF MEN EXCLUDED". The father is not named or shown. Sources: TODAY; NBC News;
   NewsNation; NBC News 2023-06.
9. **Cheek swab (K_swab, on screen only).** Prosecutors said in June 2023 that a cheek swab taken after the arrest matched
   the sheath DNA directly. Sources: Oxygen; UPI 2023-06-21; Yahoo News (titles in the extracts).
