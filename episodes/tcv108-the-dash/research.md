# Case File 108: research ledger (THE DASH · ONE SMUGGLER, told as turns, engine v11)

Built 30 October 2026. Lane: border (a Border Patrol checkpoint run). One person: the driver.

## Verdict, first

**Christopher Seabrook, 50, of Philadelphia.** On 25 July 2024 he drove a silver Chrysler sedan into the Javier Vega Jr.
Border Patrol checkpoint on US 77 near Sarita, Texas (south of Kingsville). During the initial inspection he fled and led
law enforcement on a chase that reached about 125 mph. Authorities stopped the car by puncturing its tyres; his passenger
tried to run on foot and was caught. A search of the car found 10 bundles of cocaine, 9.8 kg, hidden in the rear panels.
He pleaded guilty (22 Nov 2024) to possession with intent to distribute cocaine and was sentenced to 120 months (ten
years) in federal prison in Corpus Christi (March 2025). The passenger, Eric Barclay, 34, also of Philadelphia, pleaded
guilty (22 Jan 2026) and got 120 months too (April 2026).

Why it qualifies: one person (the driver; the passenger appears only as one line, unnamed); concluded (plea and
sentence for both men); the physical moment the v11 brief asks for is the person's own act (he floored it out of the
inspection lane, 125 mph), reported by the prosecutor's office and, independently, by The Monitor (McAllen) from the
criminal complaint; the half-reveal object (the rear trim panel the cocaine was hidden behind) is the reported hiding
place; nothing from Case Files 37–107 (not the fence Jeep, not the cannon, not a tunnel; no earlier reel uses this
checkpoint or these men: repo searched for "Seabrook", "Sarita", "Javier Vega").

## B. The story in order

1. **The drive.** 25 July 2024: a silver Chrysler sedan, two men from Philadelphia, rolls into the Border Patrol
   checkpoint on US 77 near Sarita (an inland checkpoint; its distance from the border is not narrated).
2. **The dash.** During the initial inspection, the driver floors it out of the lane.
3. **The chase.** Up the highway at up to 125 mph, with law enforcement behind him.
4. **The stop.** They puncture the tyres. The car stops (the Monitor: in a cornfield north of Ricardo). The passenger
   runs on foot and is caught.
5. **What was in the car.** Behind the rear panels: 10 bundles of cocaine, 9.8 kg (prosecutors).
6. **The outcome.** He pleads guilty (Nov 2024) and gets ten years (Mar 2025). The passenger pleads and gets ten years
   too (Apr 2026).
