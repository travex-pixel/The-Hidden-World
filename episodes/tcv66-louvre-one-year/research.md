# Research ledger: the Louvre heist, one year on (and the museum thefts that followed)

**Case File 66 · episode `tcv66-louvre-one-year` · status verified 2026-10-17 · post on 19 Oct 2026 (the first anniversary).**
All three cases are open. In the Louvre case several people have been charged (*mis en examen*) in France; nobody has been tried; everyone charged is presumed innocent. No arrest was found in the Renoir or Dalí cases. Every accusation is attributed.

This ledger builds on Case File 04's ledger (`episodes/tcv04-louvre-heist/research.md`, verified 2026-10-11). Claims re-used from it are marked **(CF04)**; everything that could have moved since was re-checked on 2026-10-17.

## Method and limits
- **Research date:** 2026-10-17. **Searches:** 15 web searches (one in extended mode), the full budget.
- **Search extracts only.** No full article, prosecutor's release or court document was read; each source's `accessed` field in `episode.json` says so. Sources marked "via Case File 04 ledger" were verified there on 2026-10-11.
- **Absence of evidence.** "No recovery reported" means no extract up to 2026-10-17 reported one, not that none happened. **Re-check on 18–19 Oct before posting.**
- **Not found:** the WSJ "year of the heist" piece and its tovima.com mirror (the "14+ museums" count) did not surface in two targeted searches. The count is **not used**. NPR (via a search summary) says "more than a dozen major art heists since January 2025"; single extract, **not used**.

## Allegations / status (attributed, not narrated beyond "suspects charged")
- **Charges.** 29 Oct 2025: two men charged (organised theft, criminal conspiracy); 1 Nov: a man and a woman (complicity) charged; 28 Nov: a fourth suspected member of the four-man team charged (Al Jazeera, France 24). Later counts differ: "five charged" (Artnet mid-2026, AFP Jan 2026 per CF04); Euronews June 2026: "four suspects now in custody". The card says only **"suspects charged, none tried"**. No trial date found.
- **The Belgian lead (June 2026).** Per Euronews (citing Le Parisien) and blue News (dpa, citing Paris Match/Le Parisien), phone-record analysis turned investigators toward Belgium; the Paris prosecutor would neither confirm nor deny. **Not narrated** (unconfirmed by the prosecutor; no space).
- **Renoir, Dalí:** investigations open (organised-gang theft, Renoir; gendarmerie research section, Dalí). No arrests found.
