# Case File 42: The Bali bombings and the van's number (tcv42-bali-van)

Research ledger, 2026-10-14.

## Method and limits
- 10 standard web searches. WebFetch and curl are blocked in this environment, so every source below is cited from **search-result extracts only**. No full page was read.
- Where the extracts disagree, the narration uses the narrowest wording that all of them support, and the disagreement is recorded below.

**Disagreements:**
- **Arrest date.** VOI gives 6 November, and CNN's "yesterday" on 7 November also points to the 6th, possibly a US-time effect. Wikipedia, CSM and CBS support 5 November. The narration says "just 24 days after the attack", which follows the 5 November date. The research disagreement is recorded here.
- **Which number.** CSM and CNN say the engine serial number; TIME and VOI say engine block and chassis; Keelty says chassis. The narration says only "its engine number had been filed off" (CSM, TIME, VOI) and "investigators still recovered the serial number" (CSM, CNN). It does not say where or how the number was read.
