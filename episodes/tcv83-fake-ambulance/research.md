## Corrections to the brief
- **Route and police force:** the fake-ambulance ring (2015) ran from the **Netherlands to Britain**, not Spain to the Netherlands. The ambulance was tracked by Britain's **National Crime Agency** from Harwich (Essex) to a scrapyard in Smethwick (West Midlands) on 16 June 2015; Dutch police then raided two yards near Amsterdam (Hoofddorp, Zwanenburg) and found about ten more ambulances. Trials were at Birmingham Crown Court (Dec 2015, Jul 2016).
- **Quantity:** no outlet reports "about a tonne" in one ambulance. The seized ambulance held **193 kg of cocaine, 74 kg of heroin** and ecstasy (RT citing the NCA; AP via Fox News), about 270 kg in all (Express & Star). One outlet (Daily Mail) says 93 kg of cocaine; the reel uses 193 kg, the figure two sources agree on.
- **"Dummy on the stretcher":** not reported. What two sources report is a gang member posing as a tourist/patient **on crutches**; that is the callback.
- **Other candidates checked and dropped:** a fake DHL/courier van with hashish in Spain (nothing found); a fake police vehicle with drugs (the Webb County sheriff-truck story is single-sourced to Jalopnik; the Odisha/Sri Lanka/Nigeria cases are single-sourced); a fake school bus (the United ISD bus, 5,408 lb of marijuana, is one NBC report with no date); a fake armoured car (nothing found); a hearse (Portugal's Operation Valhalla used a real hearse with no human gotcha; the Arizona 2017 casket was caught by a dog). Used instead: the fake **Dakar Rally** support truck (Spain, 2010) and the **coffin van** (Netherlands, 2024).

- The joke is on the disguises, never on patients, the dead or the bereaved: no body, remains or mourner is drawn; the coffins are shown empty with bags inside. No weapon, no force.
- No real ambulance service's livery: the ambulance is a plain white van with the word AMBULANCE in plain type and a plain blue/amber light bar; no cross, star or crest. The rally truck carries only plain "RALLY" lettering and generic colour stripes; no Dakar logo. The patrol car is unmarked.
