## Method and limits
- 14 standard web searches. **Disagreements recorded:**
- **Officer count.** AP: "more than 1,100 over the 23-day search" (a total across the hunt); CBS: "more than 1,200" mid-search; WTVR/CNN wire and ABC7: "more than 1,300" on 27-28 June. The narration says "1,300 officers", the peak figure, which two sources support.
- **Mitchell's plea range.** AP: 2 1/3 to 7 years; one CBS version: 2 1/2 to 7; one ABC-sourced account: up to 8. The sentencing reports (CBS NY, UPI) say 2 1/3 to 7; the card uses that.
- **Mitchell's arrest date.** CBS, CNN wire: Friday 12 June; one blog: 11 June. The card uses 12 June (day 7).
- **Distance to the border at Sweat's capture.** NPR: about a mile and a half; others: less than two miles / 2.4 km. The card says "about 2 miles".
- **Matt's death.** Early reports said he fired at a camper; State Police later said he did not fire before he was shot. The episode says only "Border Patrol shot Richard Matt dead".
- **Escape date.** The IG titles it the June 5, 2015 escape (the night); the cells were found empty on the morning of 6 June. The card says "6 JUNE 2015 · 5:30 AM COUNT".

## Opinion end question
"SHE WAS OUT BY 2020. / TOO SOFT OR FAIR?" The brief suggested "SHE SAID SHE WAS IN LOVE", but in her Today interviews Mitchell **denied** any love for Matt ("There was never any love between myself and Mr. Matt"; the "thought it was love" wording came from NBC's sources, not from her). The question was therefore built on her verified release date instead.
