# Case File 70: The finger in the Wendy's chili (tcv70-wendys-finger)

Research ledger, 2026-10-17 (all accesses on this date). A closed case: Anna Ayala and her husband Jaime Plascencia pleaded guilty in September 2005 and were sentenced on 18 January 2006. Nothing is alleged or pending.

## Method and limits
- 8 standard web searches. Every source below is cited from **search-result extracts only**; no full page was read (WebFetch was not used).
- Newsrooms counted as independent: Associated Press (as carried by Fox News, Deseret News, the Lawrence Journal-World and the Rutland Herald), UPI, Al Jazeera, NBC News, ABC News, CBS News, NPR, NBC Bay Area, News 3 Las Vegas, just-food, QSR Web; plus Snopes, A&E and Wikipedia as secondary checks.
- Where extracts disagree, the narration uses the narrowest wording all of them support.

## Corrections and checks against the brief
- **List format:** only the Wendy's case was verified in this session, so (as the brief allows) it is told as **three escalating beats of the one case**: 1 THE CLAIM, 2 THE COST, 3 WHOSE FINGER. This also avoids overlap with Case File 69 (KFC), built in parallel.
- **The accident (brief: "a truck tailgate?")**: CONFIRMED in the main. The fingertip belonged to a co-worker of Plascencia who lost it in an industrial accident in **December 2004**; AP (via Fox/Rutland Herald) and Snopes / News 3 Las Vegas describe the tailgate of a truck at the Las Vegas asphalt/paving company where they worked. One extract mentions a "mechanical truck lift" instead; the narration says only "He lost it in a truck tailgate at work."
- **The co-worker** was never charged (ABC News). He is **not named or shown**; accounts of what he knew differ (one says he denied it was his until DNA confirmed it, another that he never knew of the scam), so none of that is used.
- **The price:** court documents (as reported by AP via Fox News and Deseret/just-food) say Plascencia paid **$100** for the fingertip. Used, attributed on screen ("Court papers, as reported by AP").
- **Losses:** "$2.5 million" is the loss in the **felony complaint** (AP via Deseret: "Authorities put the Bay Area loss at $2.5 million, according to the felony complaint"; NBC News: Wendy's said it lost $2.5 million; the plea was to attempted grand theft "with damages exceeding $2.5 million"). The narration attributes it: "The charges put the loss at $2.5 million." The brief's "$2.5m+" is right; "millions" in the brief is consistent.
- **Staff:** sales fell at the area's Wendy's restaurants, leading to layoffs and cut hours (AP/Deseret; NBC News); the court ordered about **$170,000** for employees' lost wages (UPI). Narration: "Workers lost their jobs."
- **Reward:** Wendy's offered a **$100,000** reward for information on the finger's source (NBC News; Deseret/AP). An earlier $50,000 figure appears only on a forum: not used. Used in the rehook ("A $100,000 question").
- **Sentences (brief: 9 years and 12 years 4 months):** CONFIRMED. 18 January 2006, Santa Clara County Superior Court, Judge Edward Davila: Ayala **nine years**, Plascencia **12 years and 4 months** (UPI, Wikipedia; Al Jazeera, Fox/AP and ABC: "nine" and "more than 12").
- **Restitution:** about $21 million (UPI: "more than $21 million"; ABC: $21.8 million to Wendy's International and franchisee JEM Management); Wendy's agreed not to collect if the couple did not profit from the story (NPR; ABC). Not narrated (figures differ slightly); mentioned in the seed comment only as "about $21 million".
- **Release:** NBC Bay Area says Ayala was released after about four years for good behaviour; another source (single) says her sentence was reduced on appeal. Not narrated.
