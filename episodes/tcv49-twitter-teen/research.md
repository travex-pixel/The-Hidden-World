## Lesson (checklist card with SAVE ribbon, captions off) — for the viewer himself
Spoken cue: "Want to stop this? Save these three."
- "USE AN AUTHENTICATOR APP" — FTC: text-message verification may not stop a SIM swap; use an authentication app or security key (https://consumer.ftc.gov/node/76732 ; BleepingComputer on the FTC guidance https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ftc-issues-guidance-on-protecting-against-sim-swap-attacks ); CISA: SMS codes can be phished or SIM-swapped (https://www.cisa.gov/MFA).
- "SET A CARRIER ACCOUNT PIN" — FTC: set up a PIN or password on your cellular account to block unauthorised changes.
- "NEVER GIVE A CALLER A CODE" — FTC consumer alert (June 2024): sharing a verification code with a caller is always a scam; never share a verification code (https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/06/got-call-about-fraud-activity-your-bank-account-it-could-be-scammer); FBI IC3 PSA (2022): do not give PINs or passwords to callers; call your carrier back on its listed number.
