# Case File 65: The prison rats (tcv65-prison-rat)

Research ledger, 2026-10-17 (all accesses on this date). Status: the rats were found by staff in March 2019; police investigated with the prison service; no arrests were reported in any source found.

## Method and limits
- 10 standard web searches. WebFetch and curl are not used, so every source below is cited from **search-result extracts only**; no full page was read. BBC, AP, The Guardian and The Independent could not be queried from this environment.
- The rat story came from a Ministry of Justice announcement (25 March 2019) reported by CNN (syndicated by CBS Baltimore and WTVR), ITV News, the PA wire (The Scotsman, Guernsey Press) and Fox News. Counted as at least three independent newsrooms (CNN, ITV, PA) plus Fox.
- Where extracts disagree, the narration uses the narrowest wording all of them support.

## Corrections and checks against the brief
- **Place and date: WRONG in the brief.** The dead-rat case was **not a Georgia (USA) state prison and not 2016**. It was **HMP Guys Marsh, near Shaftesbury, Dorset, England**, announced by the **Ministry of Justice on 25 March 2019** (CNN, ITV, PA, Fox). The Georgia story found in search was a **dead cat** stuffed with phones near a prison fence (2012), not a rat; not used.
- **Contents:** five mobile phones with chargers, three SIM cards, cigarette papers and drugs including Spice and cannabis (ITV, Scotsman, CNN, Guernsey Press). Tobacco appears in only some reports and is not used. One Reuters-based report mentions cash; single source, not used.
- **"And it was not even the first one": FALSE.** The ministry said rats were a **first**; pigeons and tennis balls had been used before. The rehook was replaced with "With stitches. Like teddy bears." (the stitches are sourced; the teddy-bear comparison is the narrator's own joke).
- **The order of the list** follows the ministry's own framing: tennis balls (and pigeons) came before; drones are the modern volume method; rats were new.
