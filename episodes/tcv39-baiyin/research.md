## Fun fact (one card)
- **Y-STR DNA passes from father to son almost unchanged**, so male relatives share essentially the same Y profile; it
  points to a male family line, not one man. Cellmark ("all male descendants have essentially the same Y-STR profile");
  FIU GFJC ("passed down from father to son without recombination").

## Lesson (one card)
- **Your DNA isn't only yours:** one man's sample can point police to his male relatives (the same Y profile), which is
  how this cold case was reopened. Cellmark; FIU; CNN 2019 (the uncle's DNA was close enough to suggest a relative).
