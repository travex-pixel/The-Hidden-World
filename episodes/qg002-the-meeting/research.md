# THE QUIET GAME #2 — The Meeting: research and fact check

**Status:** fiction. On screen the video makes no scientific claims. The closing lines ("Everyone agreed. Nobody wanted it. They all thought everyone else did.") describe the story.

| Story beat | Idea | Strength | Source(s) consulted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everyone privately hates the plan; everyone publicly agrees; even the person who proposed it didn't want it | **Abilene paradox**: a group agrees to something no member wants because each assumes the others want it | **Plausible / descriptive.** A 1974 management essay built on an anecdote (a family drives 50 miles to Abilene that nobody wanted). Widely taught, not an experimental finding. | S1 (secondary summary), S2 (hosted reprint of Harvey 1974) |
| Each person thinks they're the only one who hates camping | **Pluralistic ignorance**: people believe their private view differs from what others think, when it doesn't | **Established** in survey research, e.g. Prentice & Miller (1993): students thought they were more uncomfortable with campus drinking than the average student. | S3 (DOI confirmed via search), S4 |
| "Someone say something…", then the first yes makes the next easier | Social influence / conformity in groups | Illustration only; the video does not cite a specific experiment for this. | n/a |
