## Method and limits
- WebSearch result extracts only (about 26 searches); WebFetch and curl are blocked in the build environment, so no article page was read in full. Every claim used on screen comes from at least two independent outlets' extracts.
- **The headline vehicle is not a mobility scooter.** The assignment asked for the best-verified mobility-scooter / lawnmower / bicycle / horse / golf-cart / forklift / bus getaway. Four searches for a mobility-scooter getaway found only (a) robberies of people riding mobility scooters (Worksop, Scunthorpe, Peterborough, the Gloucester manslaughter), where the victim would be the picture, and (b) an Isle of Wight shoplifter who hid goods in his own scooter's box while waiting for a prosthetic limb: the joke would be on a disability. Both are excluded by the brief. The lawnmower chases found (Ohio, West Virginia, Vermont, Florida) were DUI or fleeing cases with no theft, or (Aiken County SC) a robbery in which a clerk was beaten; the Nebraska and Marion County mower thefts had one outlet or no date. Golf-cart and horse cases involved guns or were single-source. The best-verified absurd getaway with a reported outcome is the **Kayseri donkey** (February 2026, sentenced May 2026, eleven outlets), so the episode id stays as assigned and the title, headline and search terms name the donkey.
- The Kayseri defendant is identified only by initials in the Turkish press (Mehmet Ç.) and is never named here; he was convicted at first instance and may appeal, so the label says "first instance". The Chula Vista man pleaded guilty and was sentenced (DOJ), so his outcome is stated; he is still a silhouette and never named. No outcome was found for the Austin 19-year-old (three searches), so he is ACCUSED only ("police say", charged, AS OF OCT 2026).

## Corrections to the brief
- **Vehicle:** mobility scooter → donkey (see above). The id tcv91-scooter-getaway is kept; `title`, `hook_title`, `search_terms`, the end card and the caption say DONKEY.
- **Kayseri date:** Turkish outlets (DHA, NTV, Karar) give 9 February 2026 at about 04:00; Gulf News says "early on February 9", Türkiye Today's syndications "February 10" in one version. The label uses 9 FEB 2026 (the majority and the agency copy).
- **Where the gold was hidden:** the English coverage says he confessed to burying it in a vacant lot; NTV's gallery of his statement says he tied the donkey in a barn and hid the gold in a hayloft; the prosecutor said his father handed it in. The reel says only that the gold was recovered.
- **The donkey:** one Turkish report says he had left the donkey about 500 m from the shop; others say he rode off on it. The reel shows him riding away and does not say where the donkey came from.
- **Austin (Jump/Uber):** the brief's not-reused list includes "the Uber" (Case File 87's ride-share car). The Austin scooter was a Jump scooter owned by Uber; the narration says "the scooter company" and no brand is drawn or spoken.
