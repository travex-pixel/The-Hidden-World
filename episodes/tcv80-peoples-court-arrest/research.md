## Allegations (attributed)
- That Smith killed Parker is the police's allegation ("police say all evidence leads to Smith"); he is charged, has pleaded not
  guilty and is presumed innocent. Every narrated reference is "police arrested", "the charge", "police credit"; the label
  says "CHARGED · Presumed innocent". Police have not stated a motive; none is suggested.
- A 46-page arrest affidavit is sealed (Court TV; WFTV); its contents (reported to include recovered text messages) are NOT used.
