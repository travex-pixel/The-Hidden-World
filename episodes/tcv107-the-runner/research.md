# Case File 107: research ledger (ONE SMUGGLER, engine v11 "told as turns"): THE RUNNER

Built 30 October 2026. Lane: customs (Border Force at a private airfield). One person, a concluded case.

## Verdict, first

**Jason Waterman, the passenger who ran from Border Force at Bagby airfield, qualifies.** In October 2015 a light
aircraft from the Netherlands landed at Bagby airfield near Thirsk, North Yorkshire. As Border Force officers arrived to
check the plane, Waterman ran off and left a suitcase holding about 7 kg of cocaine (about £1.2m) in a nearby hedgerow.
He was on the run for almost two years, in Spain. In June 2017, the morning the National Crime Agency's appeal aired on
the BBC's *Crimewatch Roadshow*, he swam from Spain to Gibraltar rather than cross the land border, and handed himself in
to Gibraltar police that evening, dripping wet. He was extradited, **pleaded guilty** on what would have been the first
day of his trial at Bradford Crown Court (18 December 2017), and was **jailed for seven years and two months**. The pilot
was tried in March 2017 and found **not guilty**.

One person, a plea and a sentence, and two physical moments by the person himself (the run from the plane, the swim to
Gibraltar), each in two or more independent sources. The half-reveal object is the suitcase in the hedge, being unzipped
by an officer's gloved hands, inside hidden.

## The story in order (told as turns)

1. **The landing.** A light aircraft from the Netherlands lands at Bagby airfield, North Yorkshire, Oct 2015 (W1). In a
   suitcase: about 7 kg of cocaine (W2).
2. **The run.** Border Force officers arrive to check the plane. He runs, leaving the suitcase in a hedge (W3, W4).
3. **Gone.** Almost two years on the run, in Spain (W5).
4. **The appeal.** The NCA's appeal airs on *Crimewatch Roadshow*; he sees it that morning (W6, W7).
5. **The swim.** That evening he arrives in Gibraltar dripping wet, having swum from Spain, and hands himself in (W8).
6. **The end.** Extradited (W9); pleads guilty on the day his trial was to start (W10); seven years and two months (W11).
   The pilot was cleared (W13).

## Naming decision

the Olive Press and Gibraltar media. He was an adult (31–33 across the reports). He is **named on a label only, never in

carries "pleaded guilty" or "wanted" (the appeal card, a statement of the 2016 record) in the same line set. **The pilot
is not named** and is mentioned only as cleared, in the fun fact. No family member is mentioned.
