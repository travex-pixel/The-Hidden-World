## Corrections to the brief and claims NOT used

- **"Glued" → "taped".** Every source says the package was taped / firmly attached to his head; none says glued. The
  hook says "taped" (a making verb in the gate's list). series.json's youtube_title still says "Glued" (not my file).
- **"Customs lifted it" → "police lifted it".** The arrest was by Spain's National Police at the airport, not customs.
- **"One of the most comical attempts"** — not found in any extract; not used. **"Sat visibly too high"** — the
  sources say "disproportionate size"; narrated as "an oversized wig".
- **Arrest date:** "end of June" (CBS/AFP) vs 18 June (ABC13, Boston 25): no day is used.
- **Prosthetic leg:** the only verified cases are Delaware traffic stops (named men, small amounts), not airports; not
  used. **Hollow shoe soles:** verified (Hong Kong 2014, Philadelphia, Atlanta 2021, Toronto 2025) but the shoes were in
  luggage, not worn; not used. **Football shirt:** not found.
- Not used: the passengers' names, nationalities beyond what the labels need, the Lisbon "5,000 doses", the Bogotá
  "94th foreigner" count (sources differ: 94 vs 98), the Natal (Brazil) and Barranquilla fake-belly cases.
