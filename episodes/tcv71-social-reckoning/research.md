## Save tip (overlay, official sources)
- FTC: ads on social media are not always vetted; prices "much, much lower" are probably a scam; scammers ask for gift cards, wire transfers, payment apps or crypto: [FTC Aug 2026](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/08/are-ads-social-media-vetted-or-checked-scams-heres-what-know); [FTC May 2024](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/05/spot-ad-social-low-low-price-brand-name-product-think-twice); [FTC Apr 2026](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/04/how-spot-top-scams-started-social-media); [FTC data, Apr 2026](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/04/new-ftc-data-show-people-have-lost-billions-social-media-scams) ($2.1 billion lost to scams that started on social media in 2025; Facebook the top platform by losses; investment scams the largest losses).
- Action Fraud: bogus celebrity endorsements in investment ads: [Action Fraud](https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/cryptocurrencyfraud); [PA via Yahoo](https://news.yahoo.com/public-warned-over-investment-scams-using-fake-celebrity-endorsements-145721988.html).
