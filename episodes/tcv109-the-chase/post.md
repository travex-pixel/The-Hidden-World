CAPTION: He hit the throttle on a Border Force chase. The beach won.
In June 2024 a Border Force cutter closed on a rigid inflatable off Suffolk: his crewmate jumped and swam, Bruce Knowles restarted the engines, ran the boat up onto a beach and jumped into the sea, and about 350 kg of cocaine was found under the tarp (NCA). He pleaded guilty and was jailed for 17 years and 3 months in May 2025; the swimmer, caught that day, got 15.
