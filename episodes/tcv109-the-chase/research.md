## Verdict, first

**The case: Bruce Knowles, 55, of Dereham, Norfolk, the man at the helm of a rigid-hulled inflatable (RHIB) caught off
Benacre, Suffolk, on 24 June 2024.** A Border Force cutter approached; the boat failed to stop; his crewmate jumped
overboard and swam for the shore; **Knowles restarted the engines and tried to flee; after a pursuit the boat ran aground
on the beach, and as officers moved in he jumped overboard too** and was caught. Towed to Lowestoft, the boat had about
**350 kg of cocaine hidden under a tarpaulin in the hull** (NCA: about £39m). He **pleaded guilty** (Ipswich Crown Court,
August 2024) and was **jailed for 17 years and 3 months** (Ipswich Crown Court, May 2025). The crewmate pleaded guilty
too and got 15 years.

Why it qualifies:
- **One person.** The reel follows Knowles: the throttle, the grounding, the jump are his acts. The crewmate appears once
  as "his crewmate" (never named in narration or on screen) and returns only for the callback's hard fact.
- **Concluded.** Both pleaded guilty and were sentenced; no appeal was found (searched 30 Oct 2026).
- **The physical moment (v11) is in the record twice over:** he restarted the engines and fled; he ran aground; he jumped
  into the sea. The NCA release, ITV Anglia, PA (Independent/AOL/Yahoo), OCCRP and the EADT/Dereham Times all carry it,
  and the NCA released the cutter's footage.
- **Half-reveal object:** "hidden under tarpaulin in the hull" is in every source: a tarpaulin being peeled back on a
  boat's deck, the bales still in shadow, is the literal object.

  37-108 (checked: no Suffolk, Lowestoft, Benacre, RHIB or chase case).

## The story in order

1. **The run in.** Investigators believe the two men took the RHIB towards French waters, collected the cocaine from a
   larger ship and headed back (K14, attributed).
2. **The spotting.** 24 June 2024, off Benacre, Suffolk: a Border Force cutter approached; the boat did not stop (K2-K3).
3. **The split.** His crewmate went over the side and swam for the shore (K4). Knowles restarted the engines and ran (K5).
4. **The beach.** After a pursuit the RHIB ran aground on the beach (K6); as officers moved in, he jumped into the sea and
   was caught (K7).
5. **The tarpaulin.** Towed to Lowestoft: about 350 kg of cocaine under a tarpaulin in the hull (K1, K9).
6. **The swimmer.** Arrested later that day (K12).
7. **The court.** Both pleaded guilty, Ipswich Crown Court, August 2024 (K10); May 2025: Knowles 17 years 3 months,
   the crewmate 15 years (K11, K13).
