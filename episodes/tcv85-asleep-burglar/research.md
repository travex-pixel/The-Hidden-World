## Corrections to the brief
- **"Fell asleep in the victim's bed":** the two sources describe a house the man had no permission to be in; the Scioto Post calls it vacant, Heritage Headlines says "a stranger's bed" and that the owner confirmed nobody was authorised to stay there. The reel says "a stranger's house" / "a house he had no permission to be in", never "the victim's bed", and never that the owner "came home".
- **Who found him:** not a resident coming home. The complainant (who shares the owner's surname; described as the owner's relative) reported an active burglary, met officers at the back door and pointed them upstairs. The owner was contacted afterwards. Narration: "Until the owner's relative walked in and called police."
- **Time of the call:** the sources disagree (Scioto Post about 12:59 pm; Heritage Headlines dispatch about 11:32 am). No time is given; "Monday afternoon" is not used either.
- **Hemel Hempstead (brief's example):** verified (Hertfordshire man, 36, found asleep in an elderly resident's spare room wearing a balaclava on 19 April; pleaded guilty 26 May; jailed 3 years 9 months at Luton Crown Court on 25 September; announced 3 October 2026: Hemel Today, GB News, Greatest Hits Radio, Yahoo UK) but NOT used: it is a second "asleep" case, and the morning's other incidents involved a crowbar and an elderly victim woken to smashed windows, which the series rules exclude.
- **Basement-stairs case (brief's example):** verified in two outlets (WWJ/CBS Detroit, WXYZ) but neither extract gives a year; Sheriff Dale Malone's statement places it in his tenure (2013-2020). No date is shown or spoken.
- **Copper-thief window dive (brief's example):** verified; the department is the Oklahoma County Sheriff's Office (Oklahoma), incident 1 October 2026, video released about 13-14 October. The outlets DISAGREE on whether he has since been taken into custody (Fox News: "quickly taken into custody"; KOCO/Gray, 14 Oct: "remains on the run"). The reel says only that the licence identified him and that charges were filed.
