# Optional painted art for Episode 002

Episode 002 already renders with code-drawn art. To give it the painted storybook look of Episode 001, make one image containing 12 vertical panels in a 4 × 3 grid, then upload it to `assets/episodes/ep002/source/panels_sheet.jpg` and run the **Render episode** workflow. The episode switches to your art automatically.

**Important: ask for no text at all.** Image generators garble lettering, and Episode 001 needed nine manual fixes. Every label and caption is drawn by the video itself.

## Style line (start every prompt with this)
> Vintage storybook illustration, hand-painted gouache and watercolour texture, warm sepia and deep navy palette, soft cinematic lighting, detailed, vertical 9:16 panel, **no text, no letters, no numbers, no captions, no signs with writing**.

If your tool can make one image with all 12 panels, ask for: *"A 4 by 3 grid of 12 vertical comic panels with thin gutters, in reading order:"* followed by the list below. Otherwise make 12 separate images and upload them as `p01.png` … `p12.png` (see `docs/ART.md`).
