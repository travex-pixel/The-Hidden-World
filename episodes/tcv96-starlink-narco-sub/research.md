## Corrections to the brief
- **Date.** "July 2025" is the ANNOUNCEMENT (2 July 2025; Maritime Executive says 3 July, the day it reported it). The
  vessel was FOUND IN APRIL 2025: La Estrella de Panamá gives 1 April near Tayrona Park; MIT Technology Review says "last
  April"; Marine Insight says April. The reel's label: "Found April 2025"; the hook label "2025".
- **How it was caught.** The extracts agree only that the navy seized it empty (no drugs; believed a trial run). The
  detection detail — a Colombian military surveillance plane spotted a 40-ft "shark-like silhouette" off Tayrona and coast
  guard boats intercepted it — is in MIT Technology Review only (Boing Boing merely summarises it). It is on a label with
  its source named ("spotted from the air (MIT Tech Review)"), not narrated. The narrated gotcha: "The navy seized it,
  empty. A test run."
- **Capacity.** The navy said 1.5 tonnes of cocaine (AFP via CBS/SCMP; El País). MIT Technology Review / Boing Boing give
  three tonnes. The reel uses the navy's figure and labels it "per the navy".
- **India, Andaman (Nov 2024): not "remotely steered".** It was a crewed Myanmar fishing trawler; six crew were arrested;
  police said the smugglers used a Starlink Mini to navigate; about 6,000 kg of methamphetamine (Reuters via BusinessDay;
  The Tribune; Baird Maritime; The Register). An AFP line in the 2025 coverage calls it a "remote-controlled vessel", which
  the Indian reporting does not support. Not used: a crewed boat, a quantity story, and a second Starlink case.
- **Miami parasitic load (Sept 2025).** Verified (Border Patrol's BORSTAR dive team's "first ever parasitic drug seizure",
  the containership Hoheplate from the Dominican Republic) but only about 8 lb of cocaine (Marine Insight, The Maritime
  Executive, NBC Miami): too small to escalate. Not used.
- **Narco-torpedo, Callao.** Verified as June 2011, about 113 kg, found by divers of the anti-drug police (DIRANDRO). "20 m
  down" is InSight Crime's figure only and is not narrated.
