# Case File 68: The $250,000 chicken heist (tcv68-chicken-heist)

Research ledger, 2026-10-17 (all accesses on this date). Status: the Memphis arrests were on 15 September 2026; everyone is **charged/accused only**; no plea, trial or conviction was found in any reporting up to 17 October 2026. The Pennsylvania egg theft (Feb 2025) has no reported arrests. The KitKat truck (March 2026) has not been reported found.

## Method and limits
- 9 standard web searches. WebFetch to freightwaves.com and wreg.com failed (DNS blocked in this environment), so every source below is cited from **search-result extracts only**; no full page was read.
- The Memphis story: WMC Action News 5 (Memphis, Gray Media; syndicated on WRDW, FOX19, KOLD, WAFB, WCAX and other Gray stations), WREG News Channel 3 (Memphis, Nexstar), FreightWaves (trade press, read the police affidavit), CCJ (trade press), The Post Millennial and Moneywise. Counted as at least three independent newsrooms (WMC, WREG, FreightWaves).
- Where extracts disagree, the narration uses the narrowest wording all of them support, and the disagreement goes on a card.

## Corrections and checks against the brief
- **Value: DISPUTED.** "$250,000" is the task force's figure (the U.S. Marshals-led Memphis Safe Task Force), reported by WMC/Gray ("officials say") and WREG. FreightWaves reports that the **police affidavit** puts the shipment at about **$110,000**, and that MPD later gave it the same value; police also listed "about $110,000 worth of stolen merchandise" recovered. So the narration attributes it ("The load? $250,000, officials say", right after "chicken heist") and the first card reads "Task force value · affidavit: ~$110K". The headline "THE $250,000 / CHICKEN HEIST" is the widely reported figure and the name the story trends under.
- **Count: 13 vs 12.** WMC and WREG: 13 arrested (one man named in the warrant + 12 others found unloading; WREG says the task force arrested everyone including the driver). CCJ and FreightWaves count 12 (charged in the affidavit list). The narration uses 13 (the two Memphis newsrooms).
- **The drug debt is an allegation** from the police affidavit: the driver told investigators he had agreed to trade his shipment to pay off a drug debt owed to one of the men arrested. Narration: "And police say the driver was paying off a drug debt."
- **KitKat date:** the brief listed 2024/2025 chocolate thefts; the big KitKat theft verified here was reported **28-29 March 2026** (413,793 bars, over 12 tonnes, central Italy to Poland). Chosen as item 3 (most absurd, most recent).
- **Not used:** 2017 Nutella/Kinder trailer theft in Germany and Italian Parmesan thefts (not needed for 3 items; not re-verified); Quebec maple syrup (Case File 51); the 2024 "Kinder chocolate" figure in the brief was not found in this form.
