Microwaves pass through glass. The perforated metal sheet in the door is the shield: its ~1–2 mm holes are far smaller than the ~12.2 cm wavelength (2.45 GHz), so it reflects them like solid metal. ## Ready answers
- **"Is it safe to stand in front of it?"** Yes. Ovens are limited by law to a tiny leak (US: 5 mW/cm² at about 2 inches), far below harmful levels, and it drops off fast with distance. Get it checked if the door, hinges or seal are damaged.
- **"What does the glass do then?"** It keeps the mess in and protects the metal screen. Microwaves pass right through glass.
- **"My 5 GHz Wi-Fi is fine?"** Yes. The overlap is only with 2.4 GHz.
