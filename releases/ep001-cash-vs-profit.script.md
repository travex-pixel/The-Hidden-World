# Narration script

[  0.00s –   5.90s] s01_hook
  VO: Meet Marigold. Her company is worth millions on paper. So why can't it pay one bill that's due today?
  CAPTION: Worth millions. Can't pay one bill.

[  5.90s –  12.75s] s02_success
  VO: Customers are buying everything. Sales are up, orders are stacking up, and the profit report says: best year ever.
  CAPTION: Sales up. Orders up. Profit up.

[ 12.75s –  19.73s] s03_catch
  VO: Here's the catch. Sell on credit, and the sale counts straight away. The actual cash arrives later. Sometimes months later.
  CAPTION: Sale recorded today. Cash arrives later.

[ 19.73s –  27.50s] s04_drains
  VO: Meanwhile, money going out won't wait. Wages are due Friday. Suppliers want paying on delivery. Stock is bought before it's sold.
  CAPTION: Wages. Suppliers. Stock. Paid now.

[ 27.50s –  35.37s] s05_reservoir
  VO: Picture a reservoir of cash you can spend today, and a long pipeline of money customers still owe you. Today's bill waits at a gate that only takes cash.
  CAPTION: Cash you have now is not the same as money you are owed.

[ 35.37s –  42.27s] s06_pressure
  VO: More invoices land. A supplier pauses deliveries. The profit report says five hundred thousand. The bank says forty.
  CAPTION: Profit: $500k. Cash: $40k. Bill due: $120k.

[ 42.27s –  56.94s] s07_revelation
  VO: Profit measures what a business earned over a period. Cash flow tracks money actually moving in and out. When the timing slips, even a profitable business can hit trouble. It's not the only way companies fail, but Australian liquidators name cash-flow problems more than any other single cause.
  CAPTION: Profit = earned over a period. Cash flow = money moving in and out.

[ 56.94s –  64.67s] s08_payoff
  VO: Then: ping. A big customer finally pays. The reservoir fills, the supplier's back, and Marigold celebrates. Briefly.
  CAPTION: A big customer finally pays: $350,000

[ 64.67s –  70.60s] s09_lesson
  VO: Lesson learned: being rich on paper is lovely. Paying people in actual money is even better.
  CAPTION: Rich on paper is lovely. Paying people in actual money is better.
