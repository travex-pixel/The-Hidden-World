## Ready answers for likely questions
- **"What if I stop at a service station?"** Stopping adds time between the two reads, so your average goes down, not up. The camera only knows the two timestamps.
- **"Do they work at night?"** Yes. They light your plate with infrared you can't see, and UK plates are retroreflective, so they bounce it straight back.
- **"Is it 10% + 2?"** Prosecution thresholds are police guidance and can vary. The camera itself just does distance ÷ time. (Don't promise anyone a buffer.)
- **"Are they always on?"** Not every camera is active all the time, and that varies by scheme. The video explains how they work, not which ones are switched on.
- **"25 million a day, really?"** That's the published estimate for UK police ANPR reads (25–35 million a day, NPCC and Surveillance Camera Commissioner). The national system is built to handle more.
