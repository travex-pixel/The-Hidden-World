Harvest, 1351. Three years after the Black Death, workers are scarce, and taking a pay rise is against the law. After fourteen hours of hard yakka, your best mate flashes his illegal wage in the alehouse, someone threatens to dob him in, and a jug comes down. Then the real horror starts: no police, a whole village forced to hunt one of its own, forty nights of sanctuary, a coroner's inquest where even the jug has to be paid for, and a long walk to Dover with nothing but a wooden cross.

What's real: the 1351 Statute of Labourers and its wage limits, excess pay going to the village's tax, the hue and cry, the coroner's inquest, deodands, sanctuary and abjuring the realm (including a 1353 case with 12 days to reach Dover) are documented (sources below). You, Jacko, Simmo, Maud and the coroner are fictional, and the Aussie slang is ours, not 1351's.
