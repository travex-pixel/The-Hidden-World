What If the Statue of Liberty Walked Home?

Look at the Statue of Liberty's right foot: the heel is off the ground. She's been mid-stride since 1886. So what if, one night, she finished the step and walked home to Paris? We follow her out of New York Harbor, along the floor of the Atlantic past the Titanic, over the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, ashore at Mont-Saint-Michel, past the Arc de Triomphe and up to the Eiffel Tower, built by the same engineer who built her skeleton. And on a little island in the Seine, someone has been waiting for her since 1937.
