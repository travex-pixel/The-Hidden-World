What If Earth Stopped Spinning for 1 Second?

At 8:00 a.m. tomorrow, Earth stops spinning. For one second. Then it starts again. Sounds harmless? You're already moving at about 1,670 km/h, and the ground is about to stop without you. We follow one ordinary Tuesday morning through the physics: what keeps moving, how hard that stop really is, a wind faster than any tornado ever measured, the oceans, the restart nobody talks about, the one person who survives, and the real reason Earth IS slowing down right now.

You get ten seconds' warning. Where do you run? Comment your city and I'll tell you how fast you were going.
