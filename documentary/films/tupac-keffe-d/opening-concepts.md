# The Tupac murder trial: opening concepts

Test question: if a stranger met this in their recommendations, would the first 10 seconds stop the scroll, and would the next 50 make them stay?
Only claims that are CORROBORATED in `evidence/claims.json` may carry the hook. Davis's statements are always his statements; Orlando Anderson is never presented as the shooter (he denied it, was never charged and died in 1998).

## A. Twenty-seven years, three hours (contrast first)

> For twenty-seven years, nobody was charged with killing Tupac Shakur. This summer, it took a jury less than three hours to convict.

- **Clarity:** high. Two numbers, one contrast.
- **Accuracy:** high. C11 (27 years, only person ever charged) and K-HOURS (AP and Oxygen: under three hours).
- **Impact:** high. The most famous unsolved killing in music, and a fast verdict.
- **Visuals:** the empty intersection at a red light at night, then a courtroom.
- **Curiosity:** high. What changed?

## B. The tape (process first)

> In 2008, a Compton gang figure sat down with a police task force and talked about the night Tupac was shot. Eighteen years later, a jury heard the tape.

- **Clarity:** medium. "Gang figure" and "task force" need setting up.
- **Accuracy:** high (C19, K-PROFFER, C47).
- **Impact:** medium-high.
- **Risk:** it opens on Davis's account before the viewer knows what the State argued or that he now denies it.

## C. He never pulled the trigger (paradox first)

> Prosecutors never said he fired a shot. A jury convicted him of first-degree murder anyway.

- **Clarity:** high. **Accuracy:** high (C49, C62).
- **Impact:** high, but "anyway" editorialises the verdict, and the legal reason (planning and supplying the gun) needs a sentence to explain.

## Chosen: A, then C and B inside the first minute

| Time | Job | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | Hook | 27 years with no charge; a jury took less than three hours |
| 10–25 s | Stakes | The man convicted is Duane "Keffe D" Davis. Prosecutors never said he pulled the trigger: they said he planned it and supplied the gun |
| 25–40 s | Escalate | The heart of the case, prosecutors said, was his own words: a 2008 police interview, TV, a memoir. Now he says he only talked because he was paid |
| 40–60 s | Reason to stay | He is due to be sentenced on 13 October and faces up to life without parole. How a 30-year-old case reached a verdict, and what the record still can't tell us |

After a minute the viewer knows:
1. **The mystery:** how the case was finally brought, after decades with no charge.
2. **Why it matters:** the killing of Tupac Shakur, and a man facing life in prison.
3. **Why the obvious answer is not enough:** the shooter was never tried; the case was built on the defendant's own stories, which he now disowns.
4. **What is coming:** the night, the decades, the tapes, the arrest, the trial, the verdict and the sentencing.

## Rejected material

- Davis's claims about Sean Combs (unproven, denied, nobody charged), the IBTimes "not off the hook" framing and Combs's unrelated conviction: not used anywhere.
- Knight's claim that Anderson was not the shooter, the bullets from Davis's home, round and wound counts, memoir quotes, the gang-enhancement outcome and specific sentencing ranges: not used (see the dossier's do-not-use list).
- Tupac's age at death: not verified in this research, so not stated.
- The sentencing outcome: the hearing is on 13 October 2026. This cut says only that it is scheduled. It must be re-checked, and a new versioned cut made, once the sentence is handed down.
