# Faith Hedgepeth: opening concepts

The trial is ongoing as of 13 October 2026. The defendant has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent; the opening must say so inside the first minute. Only CORROBORATED claims carry the hook.

## A. The note (chosen)
> In September 2012, a nineteen-year-old UNC student was found dead in an apartment in Chapel Hill. On the bed near her, police found a note, written on a fast-food bag. "I'm not stupid." And then, "Jealous." Police believed her killer wrote it.
- Clarity high; accuracy high (H-NOTE: ABC11, CBS News, Fox; profanity not spoken); impact high; visuals strong (night apartment, the bag as a document card); curiosity very high.

## B. Nine years, no match
> For nine years, investigators had a man's DNA from the scene, and hundreds of samples that didn't match.
- Accurate (H-COLD), but abstract as a first line; used as the stakes beat instead.

## C. The courtroom fight
> "The evidence is louder than the noise around it." "Where's the rest of the evidence?"
- Accurate (C42, C44) and balanced, but without the crime first the viewer has nothing to care about; used as the escalation beat.

Not used anywhere: injury or autopsy detail, sexual-assault forensics, the roommate "smudge" exchange, her medical history, any uncharged person, the 2016 DNA composite beside the defendant, the jail warrant. The defendant and the roommate are never drawn; Faith appears only as a silhouette card.
